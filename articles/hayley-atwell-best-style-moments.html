From polo elegance to red carpet grandeur, Hayley Atwell always delivers a sartorial star turn – but which is her best look yet?

From Rivals ' Helen Gordon to Beatrice in Much Ado About Nothing , Hayley Atwell has played them all. And her mega-watt wardrobe more than matches her talked-about talent

Por Edit · 1 de octubre de 2026

Is there a genre that hasn't benefitted from Hayley Atwell's powerful thespian prowess? Period dramas? Check. Superhero movies? Check. Action, comedy, West End and Broadway. Check, check, check.

Now, Edit's November cover star is set to reprise her role in the Jilly Cooper adaptation on everybody’s lips as Rivals returns to the telly box. But how is Atwell costuming her real-life appearances and soirée sightings?

Similar to her chameleon-esque acting abilities, the star shifts seamlessly from full-fantasy ballgown glamour to modern-day boardroom-ready tailoring with her own captivating individualistic appeal.

As Rivals returns, Cotswold fever hits a new high – and with Hollywood blockbusters behind her and Broadway ahead, Hayley Atwell is ruffling feathers in Rutshire as the former Mrs Rupert Campbell-Black

So, screen siren or wardrobe icon? Try both. At the Evening Standard Theatre Awards earlier this year, Atwell took home the Best Actress prize for her role as Beatrice in Jamie Lloyd’s Much Ado About Nothing shake-up. Equally regal was her shimmering Emilia Wickstead ensemble, which commanded attention towards its puffball-waisted, silver-hued silhouette at the event.

Wickstead proves to be a firm favourite of the actress, who has donned the London Fashion Week stalwart at multiple events over the years, including the Critics’ Choice Theatre Awards and the Royal Windsor Cup final. Atwell is elegant in Erdem, too, calling upon a 1920s-inspired floral bias-cut dress worn on the front row of the designer’s SS27 show last month. She’s also a fan of Giambattista Valli, opting for voluminous styles from the Italian father of frothy-chic when celebrating the launch of Mission: Impossible – The Final Reckoning alongside Tom Cruise at the Cannes Film Festival last year.
