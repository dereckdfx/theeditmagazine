Style

Role model: from fashion week to the silver screen, cover star Caitríona Balfe has always dressed the part

You might know her from Outlander or Belfast, or soon see her in Sense and Sensibility, but Caitríona Balfe's fashion prestige goes back a lot further…

Por Edit · 25 de agosto de 2026

From walking the runways for the likes of Chanel, Fendi and Louis Vuitton to her upcoming appearance as Mrs Dashwood in Sense and Sensibility, Caitríona Balfe's CV is stately, chic and brimming with roles – both on and off the screen – that the glitterati yearn for. The same can be said of her fashion credentials.

She's donned 18th-century bustles and ballgowns for Outlander, Moschino bodysuits and Miu Miu minis forMilan Fashion Week and, for Edit’s October cover, she throws on a midnight-hued Burberry jacket in the grounds of Ireland's Castle Leslie. But what does Balfe’s personal style look like when not in character? Pretty Hollywood-worthy really…

From bidding farewell to time-travel smash Outlander to playing a ‘manifesting’ Mrs Dashwood in Sense and Sensibility, actress Caitríona Balfe is a busy woman. So much so that Dora Davies-Evitt had to hunt her down on holiday at an Irish castle…

Leading lady elegance is at the core of her wardrobe: artfully draped red carpet gowns courtesy of Louis Vuitton, Dior and Armani Prive are current occurrences of her thespian-chic when on the awards season scene. Then there’s the boxy tailoring and stylish separates that become the modus operandi for when a pared-back-but-still-divinely-glamorous appeal suits the occasion.
