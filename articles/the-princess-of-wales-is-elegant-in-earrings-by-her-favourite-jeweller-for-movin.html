Royals

The Princess of Wales is elegant in earrings by her favourite jeweller for moving royal engagement

Catherine donned a pair of jewels from a designer beloved by Princess Diana as she joined some special guests in West Sussex

Por Isaac Bickerstaff · 1 de octubre de 2026

The Princess of Wales turned to a favourite designer on a heartwarming outing this Wednesday. Catherine joined the West Sussex Minibus volunteer team to pick up some elderly service users from across the village of Pulborough, including 94-year-old Stella Drewett, who received a house call from the future queen.

‘When they said, “It will be a celebrity,” I said, “I don’t know any celebrities,”’ recounted an unfazed Drewett, a retired school cook, who boarded the minibus with the Princess on the way to the weekly Pulborough and District Community Care Association lunch. Another regular, Lynda Jones, explained that the event ‘makes a lot of difference if you’ve lost your partner,’ describing Catherine as ‘a natural’.

Indeed, the Princess of Wales opted for brands she knows well for the afternoon outing, choosing reliability and comfort in a grey Jigsaw maxi coat, a new tie-neck blouse from Reiss, and a pair of Gianvito Rossi pumps in burgundy – as well as a new bag from Smythson.

Catherine kept her jewellery simple for the engagement, wearing her sapphire engagement ring with a trusted pair of diamond hoop earrings from her beloved Kiki McDonough – one of the (at least) 20 pairs of earrings that the Princess owns from the brand.

The future queen has been a fan of McDonough’s designs since her royal career began, following in the footsteps of fellow supporters including Sarah Ferguson, who turned to the designer when she became engaged to the then Prince Andrew.

‘She was suddenly thrown into official engagements, and we did some earrings and a necklace for her, which was good because the only way you got on to the map in those days was by word of mouth and magazines,’ McDonough told HELLO! magazine. Then, Princess Diana followed suit: ‘One morning I was in the kitchen making a cup of coffee and the builder who was working there said, “The Princess of Wales is knocking at the door,” and I said: “Yeah, right - it's too early for jokes.”’

The designer revealed that she was ‘very grateful’ to Catherine for her patronage, adding that ‘like Diana, she understands how she can help British brands by wearing them’. Indeed, the Princess owns a wide selection of both earrings and necklaces from the jeweller, which she has worn for events as diverse as Wimbledon, the wedding of Prince Harry and Meghan Markle, and on the red carpet.

Among the pairs of earrings from the jeweller in the Princess of Wales’ jewellery box, highlights include the blue topaz drops she wore to Royal Ascot, and the green tourmaline, green amethyst and diamond earrings, set in gold, which were designed specially for her to mark the birth of Princess Charlotte.

Queen Camilla is a fan, too: while her collection is not quite as vast as Catherine’s (few are), the Queen has turned to Kiki McDonough as part of her regular rotation, often spotted wearing a blue topaz and diamond pendant from the brand.

‘Receiving a Royal Warrant is the greatest honour of my career - something I could scarcely have dreamed of when I sat down with a pencil and paper contemplating my first design over 40 years ago,’ McDonough said in a statement after the news was announced.

‘To be recognised in this way for craftsmanship and consistency over decades feels incredibly special. A Royal Warrant is the ultimate mark of trust and excellence, built over time, and that makes it all the more meaningful.’

She added: ‘This moment feels like a celebration of a lifetime dedicated to the beauty and individuality of coloured gemstones.’ A dedication that the Princess of Wales most certainly understands.
