Edit Beauty Awards 2025: Best in Face

Dial up your skincare

Por Astrid Joss · 26 de septiembre de 2025

Make an entrance at society’s hottest parties by dialling up your skincare routine with something more potent. These clinical-grade formulas and high-performance fixes yield real results – precise, powerful and uncompromisingly best in class.

1 BEST VOLUME RESTORER

For faces feeling the sharp end of a semaglutide pen, this first-of-its-kind, plastic-surgeon-developed cream is the cavalry. Packed with hyaluronic acid, biomimetic collagen fragments, bakuchiol, amino acids and firming peptides, it coaxes back volume where it’s vanished, correcting signs of GLP-1 skin ageing, and silencing any whispers about ‘Ozempic face’. Vol.U.Lift GLP-1 4D Skin Rebound Complex, £105, Image Skincare

2 BEST TONER

This alcohol-free, glycerine-rich toner gives skin exactly what it needs post-cleanse: a gentle exfoliating sweep to smooth and brighten, plus a fragrant dose of rose- and camomile-laced hydration that calms, clears and primes the perfect canvas to soak up the next step in your regime. Active Tonic, £70, Cellcosmet

3 BEST SERVICE

When off-the-shelf skincare falls short, Klira swoops in to tackle root imbalances with a bespoke solution by celebrity dermatologist Dr Emma Craythorne. After a rigorous analysis of your collagen, melanin, sebum and barrier, you get a personalised blend of prescription-strength actives, delivered and adjusted monthly as skin visibly transforms. The Klira Special, from £59 per month, Klira

4 BEST NIGHT CREAM

Supercharge your beauty sleep with this dual-action cream that combines gentle exfoliation with polyhydroxy acid and encapsulated retinol to boost cell renewal, soften fine lines and help combat future blemishes. Nourishing ingredients lock in moisture and strengthen your skin’s barrier, so it feels plump and baby-soft by morning. Dreamy. Obagi Retinol + PHA Refining Night Cream, £100, Obagi

5 BEST Face cream

A precisely engineered formula with real muscle (90 ingredients, no less). Pop it on and, in 15 minutes, texture is refined and wrinkles softened. Over time, it acts at a cellular level to reset the skin matrix to a younger state. Exquisitely silky, it comes in a sculptural diamond-polished jar designed as an objet d’art, complete with a silver spatula for applying with lymphatic face-sculpting strokes. Life Matrix Haute Rejuvenation Cream, £1,550, La Prairie

6 BEST SUN PROTECTION

A powerful sunscreen that doubles as skin rehab for dry and mature complexions. The lightweight formula glides on with an imperfection-blurring tint that leaves skin looking flawless. Meanwhile, niacinamide visibly firms and brightens, hyaluronic acid and jojoba esters deeply hydrate, and plankton extract helps undo last summer’s sins. ZnO+ Mineral Care Firm + Replenish SPF50, £82, Sunsolve MD

7 BEST facial oil

Tackling dark spots, dullness and uneven tone with all-natural ingredients, this ayurvedic formulation revitalises skin with powerful botanical extracts and antioxidants that encourage cell renewal. Its star ingredient, saffron from 600 flowers, lends an earthy, spicy scent and delivers an instant golden glow. Kumkumadi Facial Oil, £135, Kama Ayurveda

8 BEST cleanser

The purveyor of the King’s favourite bath oil has taken a sovereign step into facial skincare. Black with rare bioactive Alpine Heilmoor extract, Olverum’s new cleansing balm smells like a Swiss mountain forest, melts away make-up and impurities, and replenishes moisture in one regal swoop. Alpine Revive Cleansing Balm, £55, Olverum

9 BEST GADGET

Feel like a Bond girl every time you fire up this laser gun, designed to reduce the appearance of wrinkles in just two minutes. It delivers targeted, painless heat into the dermis, triggering the skin’s natural repair process. This thermal energy stimulates fibroblasts (responsible for producing collagen and elastin) to ramp up production, strengthening skin’s structure and smoothing fine lines. Nira Pro+ Laser, £615, Nira

​​FOR REPAIR

Tested in space, where ageing is accelerated, Dr Yannis Alexandrides’s healing serum proved so effective at repairing damage, it graduated from post-surgery secret to global bestseller. Supercharging cellular repair, calming inflammation and restoring healthy skin after just 14 days – a giant leap for both skincare and your aesthetic. Repair Serum NAC Y², £240, 111SKIN

FOR STRUCTURE

Think of it as scaffolding for the skin – firming, lifting and reducing sagging without a needle in sight. A nine per cent peptide complex and potent growth factors boost collagen production, improve elasticity and help skin recover its youthful bounce. Multi Peptides & GF Advanced Lifting Serum, £176, Allies of Skin

FOR RADIANCE & REVITALISATION

This fast-absorbing, feather-light serum harnesses a powerful exo-liposome system that optimises oxygen delivery (crucial for skin cell metabolism and repair) and releases collagen-boosting ingredients steadily over eight hours. In just seven days, skin appears twice as firm; after one month, it’s five times smoother and visibly revitalised. Le Sérum, £97, Dior Skin

FOR INTENSE HYDRATION

A minimalist’s dream. This hyaluronic acid serum contains just 10 ingredients that contribute to deep hydration, boosting collagen for firmness and creating a silky-smooth finish. With no unnecessary extras or fragrances to risk irritation, it suits all skin types, punching above its weight with supercharged, skin-loving essentials. Serum Repair, £72, Dr Sebagh
