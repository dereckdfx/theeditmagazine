Edit Beauty Awards 2026: Best of British

For the best products close to home, look no further than the Edit Beauty Awards Best of British winners

Por Edit · 6 de octubre de 2026

Proof that the ‘Great’ in Great Britain extends to beauty? Look to heritage perfume housePenhaligon’s, founded in 1870, and the original ‘hair doctor’ brand Philip Kingsley, now on thecusp of its 70th year. Then there are modern pioneers like Trinny Woodall and Sarah Chapman.All homegrown heroes with global appeal.

1. BEST MASK

Sarah Chapman, the OG facialist to the stars, has created the ultimate clinic-like, at-home facial. It starts with a spicule-powered serum (which has a microneedling-like sensation), followed by a cooling, collagen-rich mask designed to plump, illuminate and firm. Just as if you’ve stepped out of Chapman’s Chelsea clinic.

Spicule Collagen Infusion Mask, £22, Sarah Chapman

2. BEST COLLAGEN

Collagen has entered the zeitgeist in a major way thanks to its reputation as a skin-supporting powerhouse. This powder blends effortlessly into drinks (try it with your morning coffee) without impacting the flavour or texture.

True Collagen Powder, £32, Ancient + Brave

3. BEST SPF

Whether you’re playing tennis or watching the polo, this fast-absorbing serum delivers SPF 50 protection against the damaging effects of the sun. With just a layer of this – crucially – non-greasy texture, you’ll be BSR (British Summer Ready) come 2027.

Solar Serum, £120, Skin|Cycles

4. BEST FOR SLEEP

Supercharged with magnesium and infused with lavender, chamomile and patchouli, this buttery cream deeply nourishes the skin and smells like a bouquet of freshly cut herbs. Massage it in before bed and nod right off.

Perfect Night’s Sleep Magnesium Body Butter, £40, Neom

5. BEST DEEP-CONDITIONING TREATMENT

Philip Kingsley, who coined the term ‘bad hair day’, turns lacklustre hair luminous with this intensive treatment, which he famously formulated for Audrey Hepburn. Work it in, pop on a shower cap and leave overnight for major results.

Elasticizer, £41, Philip Kingsley

6. BEST EYE RESET

Ditch the cucumber slices once and for all. For a 360-degree eye reset in just 10 minutes, this hydrogel mask delivers moisture and plumpens the skin, helping you face that morning meeting or evening drinks bright-eyed.

AntioxifEYE Eye Mask Goggles, £6, Pixi

7. BEST FOR VITAMIN C

Ready for your vitamin fix? Look to Trinny and her Naked Ambition serum, a global-first innovation that targets dullness, redness and uneven skin tone. The formula combines slow-release vitamin C with azelaic acid exosomes for best-in-class results.

Naked Ambition Azelaic Exosome + Vitamin C Serum, £69, Trinny London

8. BEST HAND CREAM

Inhale the uplifting aroma of patchouli and red berry as you smother your hands in this heavenly cream brimming with strawberry seed extract, vitamin E and oils of jojoba and macadamia. The pretty packaging makes it the most wonderfully British thank-you present.

Strawberry Thief Patchouli & Red Berry Hand Cream, £10, William Morris

9. BEST RENEWAL TREATMENT

Nothing short of iconic, this intensively moisturising balm – with a delicate aroma and no synthetic fragrance – melts into the skin, locking in hydration overnight. Wear it as a mask or a light cream and let the active ingredients do their work while you sleep, so you wake up to skin that looks plumper and feels softer.

Renewal Intensive Treatment, £120, Eve Lom

10. BEST FOR SKIN REPAIR

Bringing the seismic salmon-sperm DNA facial trend to your bathroom, this advanced skin-renewal formula contains PDRN, short for polydeoxyribonucleotide, the active ingredient in those aforementioned facials. This serum is meticulously crafted to hydrate, repair and restore that glow. Simply add a few droplets of the fragrance-free, lightweight formula before your moisturiser and revel in the results.

VIVA x PDRN Polynucleotide Repair Serum, £39, VIVA

BEST FRAGRANCES – COTSWOLDS BREEZE

Like walking in a snowdrop-blanketed woodland at the end of winter, this is fresh air in a bottle. It’s an intrinsically British fragrance – Grove England founder Laura Butler-Madden’s inspiration for the brand was her Cotswolds garden. With top notes of frankincense, chamomile and violet, here is a seasonal scent that will endure all year round.

Snowdrop eau de parfum, £140 for 50ml, Grove England

BEST FRAGRANCES – LONDON SUMMER NIGHTS

Ready to fall in love at first spritz? As British as they come, Her is a burst of red and dark berries (blackcurrant, blueberry and raspberry) with a luminous, white woody and vanilla base like a London summer’s evening. Brilliantly long-lasting, it will take you from desk to dancefloor.

Her eau de parfum, £145 for 100ml, Burberry

BEST FRAGRANCES – STATELY SPLENDOUR

This beguiling bouquet – named after the Oxfordshire palace in which Churchill was born – was originally crafted in 1902 for the Duke of Marlborough. Aromatic lavender, citrus and woody pine notes make it gorgeously seasonal and fabulously gender neutral.

Blenheim Bouquet eau de toilette, £145 for 100ml, Penhaligon’s
