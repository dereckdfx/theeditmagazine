Who checked in to the Louis Vuitton Hotel last night?

Beckhams, Gallaghers, Ladies and Marchionesses all descended on Mayfair to toast Berkeley Square's hottest arrival

Por Chandler Tregaskes · 24 de abril de 2026

Berkeley Square in spring is always fabulous. With golden rays beaming down upon the Georgian facades that surround its leafy promenade, the delights of the capital’s luxury hub continues to conjure scenes of glamour and grandeur worthy of the silver screen. Last night, as the sun went down, it was its most recent resident who rocked the eternally glamorous enclave into the small hours. London’s style elite all checked in to the Louis Vuitton Hotel…

Celebrating 130 years of the ultimate arbiters of travelling in style’s famed LV monogram, the maison’s ephemeral fashion mecca opened the doors to its immersive new luxury destination. On hand to toast its arrival? Society starlets, Britpop scions and It-girls galore.

The Marchioness of Bath looked the picture of elegance in a sleek, high-neck midnight-hued silk gown. Lady Amelia Windsor kept it cool in a collared bomber jacket and edgy graphic print trousers, and former Edit cover star Mia McKenna-Bruce looked racetrack-resplendent in a zipped-up crop-top and mini skirt combination. They are, after all, the title sponsors of Formula 1, holding court of that full-throttle-chic scene.

Ever wanted to spend a whole day with the Maison who knows how to truly travel in style? A new pop-up offers day-to-night glamour in the heart of Mayfair

Then came It-couple of the moment, Romeo Beckham and Kim Turnbull (i.e Posh and Becks 2.0). Turnbull showed of her DJ skills, filling the floors of oh-so-tastefully curated rooms with fresh beats as her heartthrob boyfriend continued to reign as the nation’s ultimate man-about-town in a striped shirt, jeans and chains.

Flutes of Ruinart and cocktails cooled with LV-printed ice cubes flowed as guests perused the covetable fashion and beauty offerings. Many queued up to have a go at the specially-made gym to show of the capabilities of the Neverfull bag – the trusty piece that can carry up to 100kg – which took the place of weights on a bench press. That’s the selfie snapshot status symbol you’ll be seeing on your feeds all day…

It was a night of glitz and chic to rival neighbour Annabel’s late-night hijinks. Louis Vuitton just landed in London. You can check out anytime you like, but that LV lifestyle never leaves.
