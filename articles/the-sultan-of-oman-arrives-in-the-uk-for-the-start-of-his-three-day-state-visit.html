The Sultan of Oman is officially welcomed to Windsor by the King and Queen as his three-day state visit begins

King Charles III and Queen Camilla joined the Prince and Princess of Wales for a grand display of pomp and ceremony to welcome the Sultan of Oman for a three-day state visit

Por Isaac Bickerstaff · 20 de octubre de 2026

The Sultan of Oman has arrived in the UK for the start of his three-day state visit.

Sultan Haitham bin Tarik al Said landed at Stansted Airport on Monday evening ahead of the official start of the state visit on Tuesday. The itinerary will include a meeting with the Prime Minister, a formal carriage procession with the King and Queen and a glittering state banquet attended by senior royals including the Prince and Princess of Wales.

The Sultan was met in London by Prince William on Tuesday morning before the pair travelled together to Windsor, where the majority of the royal engagements will take place. The Sultanate of Oman owns a number of London residences, including Nuffield Lodge, 35 Upper Brook Street, and the embassy at 167 Queen’s Gate.

The Sultan made his way to the Royal Dais on Dachet Road in Windsor, where he was officially welcomed by King Charles and Queen Camilla as the sound of a Royal Salute was fired from the Home Park and the Tower of London. The trio then joined the Prince and Princess of Wales for a carriage procession to Windsor Castle – as has become tradition in recent state visits.

State visits are traditionally hosted at Buckingham Palace, but it remains under renovation.

Upon arrival in the Quadrangle of the Castle, the royals were greeted with a Royal Salute by a Guard of Honour while the regimental band played the Omani and British National Anthems. The Sultan and King Charles inspected the Guard before all five royals viewed a military parade. This will be followed by a lunch in the State Dining Room, with other members of the royal family, and a special exhibition of Oman-related items in the Green Drawing Room.

The culmination of the state visit will come this evening, when King Charles and Queen Camilla will host the Sultan for a state banquet, with both the King and the Sultan delivering speeches. Buckingham Palace has revealed that other members of the royal family will also be attending the banquet, and in recent years, the Princess of Wales has donned a tiara to join the likes of Prince William, Princess Anne, the Duke and Duchess of Edinburgh, and the Duke and Duchess of Gloucester.

On Wednesday morning, the King and the Queen will bid farewell to the Sultan of Oman, who will then make his way to London to meet with Prime Minister Andy Burnham at Number 10 Downing Street. But there is more royal pomp and ceremony to come, as His Majesty is then invited to join the Duke of Gloucester for a Guildhall banquet hosted by the Lady Mayor.

On Thursday, the Sultan will conclude his British trip with a visit to Here East at the Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park, where he will learn more about place-based transformation and the regeneration projects following the London 2012 Olympics. After a showcase of energy and renewables, he will depart for Oman from Stansted Airport.
