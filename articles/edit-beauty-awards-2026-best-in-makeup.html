Edit Beauty Awards 2026: Best in Makeup

Insiders and socialites alike can't get enough of the products crowned in the Edit Beauty Awards Best in Makeup

Por Edit · 6 de octubre de 2026

Every socialite’s vanity case in 2026 should be full of versatile products that will last from breakfast at Claridge’s to the paparazzi flash as the night ends. Think glittering eyeshadow palettes, multi-use cream blushes and ultra-light setting spray. Just add a glossy lip and you’re ready to glide into the cab to Mayfair.

1. BEST LIP TINT

This water-like lip tint combines the comfort of a balm, the glow of a lip oil and the long-lasting colour of a tint. It includes skincare ingredients, such as hyaluronic acid for moisture and black rose extract for smoothness, making lips feel as good as they look.

Glow Oil-In-Water Tint, £36, Gucci

2. BEST LIPSTICK

Made for life, Guerlain’s Rouge G comes with a customisable, refillable jewel case of your choosing. It sets a luxurious first impression, befitting a product that feels best-in-class. The formula itself is smooth, developed with natural-origin waxes, and available in satin or velvet matte finishes.

Rouge G, The Customisable Ultra-Care Lipstick, £69, Guerlain

3. BEST SETTING SPRAY

This new lightweight mist has London talking. Noticeably less film-like than the icons of the setting-spray arena, it is designed to be long-wearing and hydrating – and it delivers. The natural finish is non-sticky and perfect for day-to-night socialising and camera flashes.

Backstage Airflash Mist, £35, Dior

4. BEST MASCARA

Lancôme has dominated the world of mascara for decades – and for good reason. Its newest release claims to have a fast-setting curl formula that can boost the curl in your lashes by up to 150 per cent, as well as amping up the volume by up to 800 per cent. Less heavy than might be expected, it builds easily: the curl and fan of the lashes are quite simply spectacular.

Lash Idôle Curl Goddess, £28, Lancôme

5. BEST POWDER

Make-up artist turned beauty mogul Huda Kattan helped catapult the ‘baking’ technique into the mainstream – and her famous setting powder? Still unbeatable. Available in 12 shades, the finely milled powder clings to a brush with ease and fixes both light and heavy make-up in place without looking cakey. It genuinely delivers an airbrush finish and is a must-have in any make-up artist or socialite’s beauty kit.

Easy Bake Loose Baking and Setting Powder, £34, Huda Beauty

6. BEST BLUSH

Hailey Bieber is the queen of beautiful, modern cosmetics that fit seamlessly into a chic life. Her Pocket Blush comes in 13 equally covetable shades. With its hydrating texture, it can be used as part of a full makeup look or alone for a more fresh-faced style. A new hero product.

Pocket Blush, £25, Rhode

7. BEST EYELINER

Perhaps the most functional eyeliner on the market, this stick includes a kajal end – smudgy, buildable and smoky if tapped out with your finger – and an eyeliner end for an ultra-precise finish. Long-lasting and immovable: a new must-have.

Intense Black 24H Kajal Eyeliner Duo, £12, Kay Beauty

8. BEST PRIMER

This primer glides onto the skin like silk, giving instant radiance and a hydrating feel. Enriched with niacinamide, a form of vitamin B3 that helps boost barrier function and improve moisture retention, it gives a sheen that acts like a light filter. This sheen lasts the whole day long under – or even instead of – foundation. The fresh peachy scent is delightful, too.

Skin Nova Instant Radiance Primer, £33, Vieve

9. BEST FOUNDATION

This long-wear foundation blends indetectably into skin but allows your natural complexion to shine through. It’s buildable and enhanced with niacinamide – which can help balance oil production, preventing those shiny patches developing during the day – and copper tri-peptide, known to boost skin health.

Hyper Bond All-Day Stay Serum Foundation, £30, Wonderskin EU

10. BEST LIP OIL

Lips are irresistibly slicked and bursting with moisture when this lip oil is applied. Givenchy has form in barely-there beauty and this new product is no different. A largely skincare-based formula, it acts like a serum and delivers a visibly plumping effect. The Iridescent Peach shade is particularly stunning.

Perfecto Serum Lip Oil, £35, Givenchy

11. BEST EYESHADOW

You can tell it’s Dolce from afar: the gold heart-shaped compact is as maximalist as eyeshadow gets. A collection of six colours – with satin and sparkling finishes – offers day-to-night glow that, when rigorously tested across a week at Royal Ascot, proves smudge-proof and photo-friendly.

Ever Icon Eyeshadow Palette, £61, Dolce & Gabbana
