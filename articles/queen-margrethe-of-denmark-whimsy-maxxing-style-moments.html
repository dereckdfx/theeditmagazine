Is Queen Margrethe of Denmark the original Whimsy-maxxer?

TikTok Youth has been yearning for more joy, finding solace in sticking rainbow-hued gems to just about anything they can. As this new Gen-Z trend continues to erupt online, could those seeking more whimsy benefit from taking a leaf out of Queen Margrethe's – very joyous – style playbook?

Por Chandler Tregaskes · 3 de septiembre de 2026

Wave goodbye to clean-girl neutrals and nights in, the season of the ‘Disgraced Socialite Aesthetic’ is here and it’s time to go out until sun-break and embrace haute hangover-chic…

Whimsy-maxxing. Verb. While you might not find this one in the Oxford Dictionary (yet), netizens have coined the term to describe the conscious act of adding moments of joy into your life. To some, it might mean something as small as stopping to smell the roses. To others, it could involve filling the calendar with extended mindful periods of playful, happy experiences - like painting little love hearts on topiary candles and hosting a ribbon-adorned dinner party full of colourful plates; popping on a frilly pinafore dress and skipping through a field of verdant green lawns; or simply sticking a pack of rainbow-hued rhinestones onto your slicked-back cheveaux - or on a cake, as Kylie Jenner did for her ‘Princess Kitty’-themed birthday party last month which set the TikTok crowd ablaze with ample, palpable whimsy-yearning on the FYP.
