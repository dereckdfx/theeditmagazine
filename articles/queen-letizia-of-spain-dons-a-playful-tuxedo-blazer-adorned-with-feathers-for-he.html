Royals

Queen Letizia of Spain dons a playful tuxedo blazer adorned with feathers for her first state visit to Finland

When it comes to regal tailoring, few royals understand the assignment better than Queen Letizia

Por Isaac Bickerstaff · 6 de octubre de 2026

Queen Letizia of Spain demonstrated her stylish flair as she kicked off her first state visit to Finland. After arriving in Helsinki on 5 October, the royal joined her husband, King Felipe, for a reception with representatives of the Spanish community in Finland and wore an ensemble that will certainly have turned heads during the meeting.

Her Majesty put her characteristic spin on sleek tailoring, wearing a Pertegaz black blazer-and-trousers set adorned with rhinestones and boasting some impressively feathered sleeves. According to the brand, the whimsical ensemble embodies a blend of ‘comfort and luxury, functionality and femininity’ – a perfect piece, then, for Queen Letizia.

Indeed, it is not the first time that the Spanish royal has donned the blazer. She first stepped out in the feathered finery at the 2019 Princess of Girona Awards, pairing it with a Karen Hallam ring and Magrit Mary clutch. Three years later, she wore it once more at an audience for the NATO Parliamentary Assembly, this time following the feather theme with Chanel’s plum earrings and completing the look with a pair of black Manolo Blahniks.

For the reception in Finland, she reused her Karen Hallam ring and added a pair of Massimo Dutti slingbacks and her ‘Amor Che Tutto Move’ ring from Coreterno. This is an essential part of Queen Letizia’s jewellery collection; the inscription, inspired by the last line of Dante’s Divine Comedy, translates to ‘Love Moves Everything’, and inside is written the phrase ‘As long as I’m existing, you will be loved’. The royal has worn the piece on almost every official outing since January 2023.

Queen Letizia gave a masterclass in smart sartorialism earlier that day, when she and King Felipe departed from the Adolfo Suárez Madrid-Barajas Airport in Madrid. The star of the show was her relaxed-fit double-breasted blazer from Hugo Boss – a beloved brand of Her Majesty’s – which she paired with a blouse from Mango, a nice patriotic nod to Spain on her trip abroad. Throughout 2020, the Hugo Boss blazer was in regular rotation, but it has not been seen since December 2025. Queen Letizia completed the look with her two rings, some heeled loafers from Massimo Dutti, and a pair of palm-inspired Isabel Guarch earrings.

Perhaps more than any other royal, Queen Letizia is known to favour tailored looks – and has done so ever since her days as the Princess of Asturias. After announcing her engagement to the then-Prince Felipe on 1 November 2003, Letizia proved that she understands the assignment when it comes to regal power dressing.

Case in point? Her meeting with President José Luis Rodríguez Zapatero, five days before she tied the knot with Felipe, or her outing to the closing of the European Association for Cancer Research in July 2012, when she paired a Felipe Varela white suit with an emerald green blouse. She even wore a white suit when she and the then Prince of Asturias announced their engagement.

The white suit combo remains a favourite, with Queen Letizia wearing a similar look in April at the Spanish Chamber of Commerce in Madrid to celebrate the 10th anniversary of the Young Talent programme, which seeks to improve employability outcomes for people aged between 16 and 29.

Queen Letizia, who has been known to embrace her silver-streaked hair of late, wore one of her favourite brands for the outing, turning once more to Hugo Boss with an orange floral blouse, which she wore underneath a trouser suit from Mirto.

No doubt royal watchers will be granted another glimpse at her enviable ensembles during the remainder of the Finnish state visit, which will see Queen Letizia and King Felipe visit Helsinki and Rovaniemi from 5-7 October to discuss the key pillars of collaboration between the two nations.
