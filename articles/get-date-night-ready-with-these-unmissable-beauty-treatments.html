Beauty

Get date night ready with these unmissable beauty treatments

Whether you're looking for a bouncy blow dry that last for days or body contouring to give you an instant lift, these practitioners more than deliver

Por Edit · 13 de febrero de 2026

Best for a blow dry: Neville Hair and Beauty

Occupying all five-floors of a converted townhouse on Pont Street, the smart Belgravia set flock here to have Neville Tucker’s team tend to their tresses. It’s like a brilliant beauty family, clients are loyal and won’t go anywhere else. Their real skill is making you and your hair look expensive. Which means a blow-dry that lasts for days (and that won’t flop as soon as you leave the salon).

Best for rejuvenated skin: Dr David Jack x Liberty

Gliding through Liberty, knowing that you have an appointment at Dr David Jack’s facial clinic – discreetly tucked just below the perfume hall – feels glamorous. Heralded as ‘a new era for in-department-store aesthetics’, it marks the store’s first-ever permanent clinic. The signature treatment? The PDRN ‘Salmon Sperm’ Reset Facial – a three-step process promising stronger, smoother, brighter, and more hydrated skin.

It begins with the Peptide 2-Phase Peel, which feels like your traditional facial but I underestimated just how transformative it would feel to have excess fluid gently worked out of my face – instantly depuffing and sculpting. Step two is microneedling with a PDRN infusion. Momentarily uncomfortable beneath the eyes, it sounds far more abrasive than it actually is and proves more than worth it. The skin-repairing benefits were visible almost immediately, particularly on the acne scarring on my cheeks. The treatment concludes with a cooling modelling mask, after which you re-emerge onto Liberty’s floors with skin as soft as a newborn’s and the satisfaction of having found the store’s most luxurious new secret.

Best for extensions: Beauty Club London

If you and your hair haven’t already discovered Beauty Club London, discover the hotspot where colour and cocktails combine. Tucked away behind Oxford Circus, this buzzing beauty mecca and one of the best hair salons in London specialises in hair extensions and colour services with a fully-stocked coffee/cocktail bar to boot. For some of the best hair colour in London, we recommend booking in with in-house balayage pro, Shady Harb. The ultimate in precision and speed, the colourist has a waiting list thanks to his natural-looking balayage blends. Specialists for tape in hair extensions, Louise Bailey and Moe Harb are the in-demand duo behind the best tapes in town to boost hair volume or length (or both). A one-stop shop for beauty, we’re obsessed with the nail bar, Paint Nails, where you can sip on a margarita or mojito while you’re having your mani.

Best for hairless beauty: Braun IPL Skin i-expert Pro 7

Now here we have a game-changer. Forget the monotony – and relentless – of endless, daily shaving rituals. Here we have a new gadget that will prompt salon-worthy results. That’s Braun IPL’s brand sparkling new Skin i-Expert Pro 7. The IPL stands for ‘intense pulsed light’ and it’s a technology that sends targeted wavelengths into the skin, to derail those pesky hair follicles. The original tech of the Pro 5 has been refined in the Pro 7 for faster results, and AI – let’s call it a ‘personal beautician’ – is used to help you keep track, proffering a little extra, very welcome hand-holding. The device links to your phone then sets up a programme tailored to your skin tone, target areas and hair colour and density. By three sessions, you will see a 95% hair reduction. Let’s just say you’ll be date-ready, without wasting any time leaving the house. You’ll never look back. Voila.

Best for an instant tone: Body contour massage at The HVN

Who doesn’t want to feel a little leaner, a little more toned, before a date? Enter the body contour massage at The HVN, an innovative, non-invasive treatment that improves skin elasticity and promotes lymphatic drainage through a mix of hands-on techniques that stimulate circulation and encourage the elimination of toxins. No surgery required!

Best for nails: 14 Day Mani via Secret Spa

There's nothing more luxurious than a manicure, but what happens when there's no time to pop to the salon? Fortunately, date-night darlings can treat themselves to long lasting nails with a 14 Day Mani via Secret Spa. Like all good manis, the process starts with meticulous nail prep, followed by 14 Day Mani base, colour and top coat – all in the comfort of your own home.

Best for complexion perfection: FaceGym Ultimate Lift and Glow

Seeking the last word in lifted, glowing skin? At the Surrenne spa and health club spanning four subterranean floors below Knightsbridge, FaceGym’s most elite treatment promises the world – and more than delivers. As you lie under blankets on a heated bed, the 90-minute facial begins with a collagen balm cleanse. Then comes the ‘gym’ part: a deep-tissue facial massage, where the therapist flicks and pummels every muscle in your cheeks, jawline, temples, forehead and neck, which results in a hot, ironed-out feeling and leaves your skin in a boosted, naturally oxygenated state, primed for the technology bit. Radiofrequency is a prickly, tightening sensation that stimulates the skin and instantly increases firmness. This is followed by an Oxypod, which feels as if your face is being lightly sanded – but in the most extraordinarily luxurious manner, with gold flakes and, crucially, a copper antioxidant. FaceGym’s Pro EMS sculpting tool uses electric currents to contract the facial muscles (a surreal sensation) and to encourage the slicked-on layers of hyaluronic and collagen serums to take effect. Lastly, a choice of microneedling or an oxygen gun ensures that these hydrating products penetrate deep into the skin. The result is an instant lift, chisel and deep glow. Winter dullness? Defeated.

Best for at-home glam: Josh Wood

Having delivered his A-list hair and beauty services to the equally A-list likes of David Bowie, Elle Macpherson, Kylie Minogue and Victoria Beckham over the years, Josh Wood is preparing to send his own hand-picked glam squad directly to you. A team of creatives who are the best in the business is dispatched to your doorstep to get you red-carpet ready. This brigade can do it all: from flawless make-up to exquisite brows; elevated mani-pedis; tinted and tidied hairlines; big, bold blowdries and elegant updos. Whatever your black-tie beauty request may be, they will execute it in record-breaking time. The best part? They will come to see you anywhere, from London to Edinburgh, or New York to Beijing. The ultimate in luxury is having this team at your beck and call.

Best for massage: Alphonsus Modebe

A masseuse who feels like a healer, Alphonsus Modebe is a true master in his field. With charismatic charm and humour, and an innate ability to read and connect with a client’s overall being, he is quite simply the best of the best. His bespoke and deeply compassionate approach provides pain relief and long-lasting mental and physical restoration to those he takes under his caring wing. For Modebe, treatments are a ‘team effort’ via which he aims to awaken clients’ connection to, and understanding of, their own bodies in order to drive self-improvement for greater, longer-lasting results. He employs a variety of techniques, including varying degrees of pressure, types of rhythm, modes and intensities of application in order to achieve what he intrinsically understands each client of his needs – anything from an improved immune system to reduced stress. His ace card lies in the selection of music he plays during treatments. From a collection of classical, jazz, R&B, samba, Afrobeat and bossa nova, he hand-selects melodies to match the rhythm and fluidity of his massage stroke – and your own body’s requirements.
