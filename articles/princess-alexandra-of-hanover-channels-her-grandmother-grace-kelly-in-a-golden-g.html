Royals

Princess Alexandra of Hanover channels her grandmother, Grace Kelly, in a golden gown from the royal archives

The newly engaged daughter of Princess Caroline dipped into the Grimaldi archives to find a dress donned by Princess Grace in the '60s

Por Isaac Bickerstaff · 21 de septiembre de 2026

The scions of the House of Grimaldi often pay sartorial tribute to the eternal style of Princess Grace of Monaco – but few are actually able to wear a gown from Grace Kelly’s wardrobe. Enter Princess Alexandra of Hanover, who celebrated her engagement by donning one of her grandmother’s most glamorous dresses.

The 27-year-old bride-to-be – who revealed last Friday that she will be tying the knot with her boyfriend, Ben Sylvester-Strautmann – slipped into a Givenchy number for a characteristically chic outing on Friday. Taking to Instagram, Princess Alexandra shared some photos from a marbled lobby, as well as a casino table and bathroom mirror, showcasing the glorious scoop-neckline gown, previously worn by Grace Kelly for the pre-wedding gala of King Juan Carlos and Queen Sofia of Spain in 1962.

‘Got to wear my grandmother’s dress from the ‘60s,’ the Princess wrote in her Instagram caption: ‘Yellow mood in archival Givenchy.’

Friends and family were quick to compliment the daughter of Princess Caroline. Alexandra Leclerc, whose girl gang of F1 WAGS regularly rub shoulders with the Monaco royals, commented a series of heart-eye emojis, while Beatrice Borromeo described her cousin as ‘divine’.

Could this glamorous moment of familial inspiration be a hint that Princess Alexandra might pay tribute to Grace Kelly at her own royal wedding? The Front Row favourite, who has recently hinted that she might follow in the literary footsteps of sister Charlotte Casiraghi, announced her engagement to the world with an Instagram post, simply captioned: ‘The marriage plot.’

Alongside snapshots of what appears to be a beautiful engagement brunch (and more than one picture with her beloved dachshund, Eloise), Princess Alexandra treated fans and followers to a glimpse of her engagement ring, a custom piece with a secret nod to her love story with German scion and former basketball player Ben.

Instagram content

Designed by Antinori di Sanpietro, the 18-carat yellow and white gold engagement ring boasts a 4.8-carat Fancy Yellow diamond, chosen by Strautmann himself, framed by two trillion-cut diamonds. The royal groom-to-be has had plenty of time to perfect his sense of Grimaldi-approved aesthetics since he went public with his princess in 2017, so the mammoth gemstone did not disappoint.

The ring bears a secret design beneath its setting, incorporating a motif inspired by the geometric patterns of the Piazza del Campidoglio in Rome, as well as inscriptions reading ‘Antinori’ and ‘Made in Italy’ – references to the royal couple’s love of the city.

Should Princess Alexandra seek to channel Grace Kelly in her wedding ensemble, she could do worse than borrowing some of the Hollywood legend’s most regal jewellery. Grace Kelly owned a dazzling diadem, set with 256 diamonds, which was donated to her daughter, Princess Caroline, for the ball held before her first wedding to Philippe Junot in 1978. The tiara has not been seen in public since the death of Princess Grace in 1982, however. So, should Princess Alexandra choose to wear it on her own wedding day, it would be a historic moment.

Another jewel from the collection of the legendary Princess Grace is her Cartier jewel necklace, which was a wedding gift from Kelly’s husband, Prince Rainier III, in 1956. There is precedent for Grimaldi scions to wear the piece to mark their own nuptials, as Charlotte Casiraghi did when she tied the knot with Dimitri Rassam in 2019.

Few could pull off these storied jewels, but Princess Alexandra of Hanover has certainly proved that she has the credentials to continue her grandmother’s legacy in style.
