Olympians, Wimbledon icons, aristos and euro-royals: Who was in the trenches at Burberry’s London Fashion Week show last night?

Pomp and Pleasantry! Burberry is back with a sunny disposition for spring. But who made the cut on the coveted front row at the Chelsea College of Arts?

Por Chandler Tregaskes · 22 de septiembre de 2026

It’s officially Burberry season. In the British heritage luxury house’s 170th year, the Royal Warranted weatherproofer-turned-arbiter-of-English-chic has scored some big points in the style stakes as of late. Yesterday, the V&A opened the doors to its own mini-exhibition focusing on the house’s most indelible silhouette - the trench coat - two years ahead of the South Ken institution’s reopening of the Fashion Gallery, which will be dubbed the ‘Burberry Gallery’ in 2028. The house also reported a 5% increase in revenue in the first quarter of 2026. And that’s not to mention the It-girls, ingenues and Hollywood icons returning to the house once again to claim that Burberry - and its home nation of Great Britain - is finally cool again. If you ever needed the proof, the Princess of Wales proudly donned a reportedly brand-new chocolate-hued trench up in Scotland just last week.

Ahead of today’s London Fashion Week show, the V&A opens the doors to a new showcase focusing on Burberry’s most famed silhouette, the trench coat

So, as the Nova-checked atelier closed SS27’s London Fashion Week schedule last night at the Chelsea College of Arts, what will become the Burb’s must-haves as we look towards the season ahead? Chief Creative Officer Daniel Lee called for a ‘Return to the basis of things. To something instinctive. To something felt. Something true.’ Keep calm and carry on, some might say…
