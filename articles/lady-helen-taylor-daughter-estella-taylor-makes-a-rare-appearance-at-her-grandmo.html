Royals

Lady Helen Taylor's daughter, Estella Taylor, makes a rare appearance at her grandmother the Duchess of Kent's memorial concert

Estella, 21, has inherited her mother's eye for fashion, but prefers to remain out of the royal spotlight

Por Isaac Bickerstaff · 7 de octubre de 2026

A series of new photographs from the Duchess of Kent’s memorial service at Sinfonia Smith Square gave a glimpse of the late royal’s rarely seen granddaughter, Estella Taylor. The 21-year-old sat next to her mother, Lady Helen Taylor for the moving concert, organised in honour of one of the late Duchess’s most treasured patronages.

Estella and Lady Helen, 62, took a seat next to her grandfather, the Duke of Kent, who turns 91 this week. The trio were in the front row for the concert, which was organised by Future Talent, the charity co-founded by the Duchess of Kent in 2003 to provide funds to young musicians from less advantaged backgrounds. In one sweet photo, Estella can be seen wearing a chic electric blue coat and hoop earrings, sitting between her mother and her uncle, Lord Nicholas Windsor.

Estella’s other uncle, the Earl of St Andrews, was also in attendance with his daughters, Lady Amelia Windsor and Lady Marina Windsor. Lord Frederick Windsor, the Duchess’s nephew, attended alongside his wife, Sophie Winkleman – the couple announced their joint patronage of the charity Wild Arts earlier this year.

He’s the grandson of Grace Kelly, the eldest son of Prince Albert II of Monaco, and for 20 years he has kept his life private. But what happened when Alexandre Grimaldi went public?

The gathering of the Kent branch of the royal family en masse was a testament to the warmth with which they remember the Duchess. Estella in particular prefers to stay out of the royal spotlight and is only occasionally spotted in the public eye. As a child, she joined the likes of the Princess of Wales on the balcony of Buckingham Palace for Trooping the Colour.

More recently, she joined her brothers, Cassius and Columbus Taylor, for the Princess of Wales’ ‘Together at Christmas’ carol service at Westminster Abbey last December, arriving alongside Lady Helen and Lady Marina.

She has also been sighted at Wimbledon, sitting in the royal box next to her mother last year in a chic pair of yellow sunglasses and a blue polka-dot dress, and she often joins the royal family for the King’s Christmas lunch at Buckingham Palace.

Clearly, Estella has inherited Lady Helen Taylor’s eye for style. A former muse for Armani, the Duchess of Kent’s daughter earned a reputation as the most fashionable member of the royal family. Armani dressed Lady Helen for much of her adult life. ‘By the late ’80s, when I was in my mid-20s, I remember thinking my style had veered too far down one path and it was time to rein it in (did I really need to wear so many puffball skirts?)’ she later recalled.

Soon, she would be an Armani ambassador, joining ‘Mr Armani’, as Lady Helen always referred to him, on front rows around the world. ‘We were in Paris for the opening of an Armani café somewhere in Saint-Germain and at the dinner afterwards, Mr Armani, Karl Lagerfeld and I had to climb over a table to reach our seats, which the two legendary designers found hilarious,’ she recalled of one afterparty. ‘It might have been the most animated I’d ever seen him at a party.’

When Lady Helen tied the knot with her art dealer husband, Timothy Taylor, at St George’s Chapel in 1992, tradition dictated that she must wear a British designer for the occasion. She opted for Catherine Walker, but on a visit to Via Borgonuovo, she watched on as Armani picked out her ‘going away’ dress and a mint-green ensemble for the Duchess of Kent.

Cayetano Martínez de Irujo, 4th Duke of Arjona, was supported by his aristocratic family as he exchanged vows with Bárbara Mirjan on 7th October 2025

After decades of working alongside Armani (who addressed her as ‘Cara Helena’), the pair went their separate ways in 2008. On 4 September 2025, both Giorgio Armani and the Duchess of Kent died, at the ages of 91 and 92, respectively. It was, Lady Helen later reflected, a ‘strange heavenly design’. She was a pillar of support for her father, the Duke of Kent, at the Duchess’s funeral, wearing her mother’s sapphire-and-diamond engagement ring at the intimate family vigil.

In Lady Helen, then, Estella Taylor has the perfect blueprint for how to live a gilded life while remaining only on the periphery of the royal fold. Could fans be seeing more of this glamorous royal scion?
