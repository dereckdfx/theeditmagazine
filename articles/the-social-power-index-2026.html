The Social Power Index 2026: introducing the 101 most socially significant people in Britain

Charisma, cachet and serious connections, these power players are the cream of the crop. Now, as they gallop into the summer season, Edit shares the social thoroughbreds you need to know – with the entire list published online in full for the first time, exclusive to members of the Edit Inner Circle

Por Sacha Forbes · 2 de julio de 2026

In the year of the fire horse, the social thoroughbreds on Edit’s power list are leading the field with charisma, oomph and that certain something that marks out the socially potent. Some also have talent, such as Dame Tracey Emin, doyenne of the YBAs, whose landmark exhibition is the culturati’s must-see this summer. Or Callum Turner – hotly tipped to be the new 007 – and his stadium-filling wife, Dua Lipa. Then there’s the leader hitting her stride in a world of political instability: Kemi Badenoch, supported by husband Hamish. Meanwhile, major philanthropists Sir Lloyd and Lady Dorfman are elevating the nation’s cultural life, most recently seen flanking the King at the Royal Opera House. But the top spot on this year’s list goes to Peter and Harriet Phillips, the House of Windsor’s newest union. These are the people who get asked everywhere that’s anywhere. Enjoy their company, because they’ve got it, all 101 of them – 2026’s cream of the crop.

1. Harriet and Peter Phillips

The Royal Family’s one-time most eligible bachelor married NHS nurse Harriet in the Cotswolds village of Kemble this summer. Find them on the circuit, enjoying each other’s company at Wimbledon, Badminton and the Royal Charity Polo Cup. Just days after their romantic nuptials, the new Mr and Mrs Phillips graced Royal Ascot – in the company of King Charles III and Queen Camilla in the carriage procession, no less. They're the new royal couple to watch.

2. Viscount and Viscountess Astor

Society’s beloved matriarch and her husband, William, celebrated their golden wedding anniversary this year. The OKA founder and her eternally youthful swain host the great and the good at their stunning home on the Scottish island of Jura in August, and it’s the invitation everyone wants. Max Hastings still sighs at the memory of Annabel in a fur coat rowing across the loch.

3. Poppy Delevingne and Archie Keswick

If Mayfair needed a poster couple for polished social glamour, it would be Poppy and Archie. She brings the fashion pedigree, he brings the dynasty gloss. Her recent 40th birthday in Marylebone's 77 club was the talk of the town. After all, what other socialite could boast Taylor Swift on her guest list?

4. The Duke and Duchess of Roxburghe

Educated at Eton and Newcastle, and with a stint in the Household Cavalry under his belt, Charles has traded sabres for stewardship at Floors Castle, with glamorous Annie at his side. Away from Scotland, Alley Cats on the King’s Road is their local London spot for pizza with close friends Violet von Westenholz and Archie Seymour.

5. Stanley Tucci and Felicity Blunt

Instagram content

The Met Gala, The Devil Wears Prada 2 premieres in New York and London… If there’s a red-carpet event, it calls for this Oscar-nominated actor and literary agent. Felicity, also an executive producer on Rivals, looks after high-powered authors, from Claire Keegan to Elizabeth Day, at Curtis Brown. At Ascot the couple were spotted arriving alongside Lord and Lady Cavendish in the carriage procession. Talk about #couplegoals...

6. Sir Lloyd and Lady Dorfman

It could have been a scene from Bridgerton: Sir Lloyd and Lady Dorfman flanking the King in the Royal Box as new stage curtains bearing his cypher were unveiled at the Royal Opera House this May. They're philanthropists and bring grand glamour to any social occasion – Sir Lloyd always dresses immaculately in a Savile Row suit.

7. Dame Tracey Emin

Recently spotted rubbing shoulders with the Earl and Countess of Rosslyn at the London Print Fair, the former enfant terrible of British art is now firmly in the inner circle. Despite mega success, Emin's still devoted to her native Margate, where long-standing friend Madonna visited her earlier this year. The queen of pop's verdict? Margate's ‘a dream’.

8. Kemi and Hamish Badenoch

Kemi has found her stride, handling hecklers with firm eloquence and taking on Rachel Reeves in the House of Commons. She says it wouldn’t be possible without her. husband, Hamish, who makes most of the money and does most of the housework.

9. Dua Lipa and Callum Turner

The pop princess and the man tipped to be the next James Bond first tied the knot in an intimate ceremony at Old Marylebone Town Hall before jetting off to Sicily to celebrate with friends and family with a wedding that took over Instagram. (If you haven't seen their honeymoon pictures, you've been living under a rock). According to their astrology charts, they’re the A-list’s most compatible couple.

10. The Hon Harry and Clodagh Herbert

These two live life at a fast pace. When not at Ascot, Harry, chairman of Highclere Thoroughbred Racing, and his wife, the vivacious chef, author and broadcaster Clodagh, can be found holidaying with the Fennells and Richmonds in Greece.

The following social swans and scions are listed in no particular order of dinner party prowess…

Beatrice Vincenzini and India Warrender

The most glamorous mother and daughter on the circuit. Both impeccably dressed, they move in fashionable circles with Giancarlo Giammetti and Elizabeth Hurley. Beatrice is a publisher (Vendome Press), and 24-year-old India is a PR associate at Zegna and close friends with Damian Hurley. In summer, you’ll find them in Greece, swimming in the turquoise coves of Spetses.

Indhu Rubasingham

The play’s the thing for Indhu. She’s the first female artistic director of the National Theatre and so dedicated to the boards that her career has done nothing but rocket skywards. And why not? All the world’s a stage. Her most recent triumph is The Misanthrope starring Sandra Oh.

Major George and Madeleine Packe-Drury-Lowe

Newlyweds George and Madeleine are the country-glam couple society can’t stop talking about: an Army major and an equine artist, dividing their time between Grade I-listed Prestwold Hall, Mustique and St Moritz, with hunting, black-tie balls and the Cresta Run in the diary.

Dame Kristin Scott Thomas and John Micklethwait

After a secret wedding in Rutland in 2024 (complete with coronation chicken), Kristin and Bloomberg News editor-in-chief John are the toast of the town. Spot them at The River Cafe (Ruthie Rogers is a close friend) and National Portrait Gallery galas with David Ross. Most recently it was an evening out to the Serpentine Summer Party.

The Duke and Duchess of Beaufort

Famous for hosting the Badminton Horse Trials, Georgia and Harry are widening their scope with the RHS Flower Show at Badminton this July – a perfect fit as the Duchess is passionate about her garden, particularly her tulips. Will Harry’s band, The Listening Device, play?

Joe Nickols

From organising Eton’s first-ever fashion show (attended by Dame Vivienne Westwood) to co-curating the five-star Samurai show at the British Museum, is there anything Japanese-fluent Sasakawa scholar Nickols cannot do? He’s front and centre of the next generation of curators.

Declan Rice and Lauren Fryer

Declan shrugs off full-backs as suavely as he lifted the Premier League trophy this season. The Arsenal and England midfielder – currently making a star turn at the FIFA World Cup – briskly rebuffs those who troll his childhood sweetheart, Lauren. He collects sneakers, loves dogs and says he has ‘no brains whatsoever’ off the pitch. Share a table with him and find out how wrong he is.

Lucas Stewart and Dylan Stewart

The Stewart boys are following in the footsteps of their grandfather, three-time F1 champion Jackie. While motorsports media strategist Dylan travels the globe attending Grand Prix races, Lucas interviews mega characters from the world of motorsport – only on a golf course. Catch them trackside.

Rosamund Pike and Robie Uniacke

Thanking Robie in her Olivier Awards acceptance speech for Inter Alia, Rosamund caused a stir by calling the businessman her ‘husband’ for the first time. Better brush up on your Mandarin for dinner at their place: sons Solo and Atom are fluent.

Cruz Beckham and Jackie Apostel

Cruz is selling out gigs with his band as Brazilian-German Jackie supports stageside. They move between west London and the Cotswolds to have dates with Romeo and Kim Turnbull. Jackie can count her would-be father-in-law, David, as a fan – the trio were snapped taking in the action at a World Cup game together.

India McTaggart and Leo Maclehose

Telegraph correspondent India and tech founder Leo live in Notting Hill, where they dine at The Fat Badger and dance at friend Steven Shamoon’s The Good Oak. There’s an open invitation to India’s godfather Tomasz Starzewski’s flat for the best chicken, as well as her family’s castle in the Highlands and Leo’s family home on the Côte d’Azur.

Johnny Macaulay

The litigation lawyer turned interior designer (for MacAulay MacAulay, with his sister, Rosy) has transformed his four-floor former embassy building in Edinburgh’s West End into a party palace. Jazz nights and cocktail parties? Drumsheugh House is where it’s at.

Daisy Knatchbull and Giovanni Damiani

From London to Palm Beach, they all love Daisy’s Knatchbull tailored suits. Her betrothed, Italian music executive Giovanni, proposed on a family holiday in the Bahamas. Everyone who’s anyone craves a seat next to him at dinner.

Sir Matthew and Lady Pinsent

Romance blossomed at Oxford for Matthew and Demetra, who divide their time between London and Sussex. The broadcaster and Olympic rower is also a steward at Henley. Economic powerhouse Demetra recently stepped down as CEO of Charlotte Tilbury – stand by for her next big move.

Ivor Braka and Elizabeth Williams

Art dealer, modern intellectual and Norfolk pub owner Ivor shares his Grade II-listed, five-storey northern-gothic house in Chelsea with glamorous American girlfriend Elizabeth. With a fridge full of alcohol and no food, he reveres King Charles’s style and once gave a retro fishing boat to Jerry Hall.

Oliver Peyton and Charlie Polizzi

Former nightclub entrepreneur (Raw), restaurant whizz (Atlantic Bar & Grill) and, by marriage to Charlie, relation to hospitality royalty, now Oliver’s the funeral director in town (his company’s called Exit Here) and the only one cycling around in a Comme des Garçons suit.

Dame Susan and Gary Langley

State trumpeters and ceremonial garms are de rigueur for Susan, the First Lady Mayor of the City of London. She and her husband, Gary, are the current residents of Mansion House, and she is second in importance only to the Sovereign when inside the boundaries of the Square Mile.

Guy and Jacqui Ritchie

Guy and Jacqui remain the ultimate countryside power pair: between shooting parties, black-tie weekends and Hollywood premieres, they inject a scintillating buzz wherever they go. The prize invitation is a weekend at Ashcombe. Also on the guest list? The Beckhams and Jason Statham.

Alison Oliver and Josh O'Connor

It's not easy to steal a scene from Margot Robbie, but Irish actress Oliver managed it as Isabella in this year’s Wuthering Heights. Don’t expect to find her posing with partner O’Connor on the red carpet, though. He, star of The Crown and now leading man in Hollywood, prefers making ceramics to the spotlight.

Yalda Hakim and Abed Rashid

She’s the Sky News anchor with the wits, smarts and mettle to handle war zones, world leaders and royals. He’s her businessman husband, and they live in south-west London. Find them at Kensington Roof Gardens – or on date nights at At Sloane and SNL UK.

Harry and Rose Bromley-Davenport

Interior-design superstar Rose and Capesthorne heir and private-equity expert Harry returned from New York in June to retake Cheshire – just in time for the British social season and to be reunited with pals including Lily Coleman and the Hon Nancy Morrison.

The Hon Charles and Elaine Astor

Belgian princess Elaine is no mere socialite. Last summer, the social crowd flocked to Hever Castle, Anne Boleyn’s childhood home and one-time seat of the Astors (husband Charlie is a scion of the dynasty), to see her musical production of the Tudor queen’s life.

Robin Birley

Britain’s most powerful transatlantic non-New Yorker has opened Maxime’s across the pond to great acclaim. In London, he hosts friends Tony Bromovsky and Isabella Cawdor for power lunches at his Oswald’s club. What’s next for Robin, widely respected for his impeccable style? A Palm Beach opening?

Noah Jupe and Jacobi Jupe

The Jupe brothers stole the nation’s hearts as co-stars in Hamnet. Noah cried at the film’s premiere, watching little brother Jacobi’s performance in the titular role (spoiler: he dies). To which Jacobi reacted by saying, ‘Oh, I made you cry-y!’ in a whiny, sibling-taunting voice.

Lord and Lady Rathcavan

The peer also known as François O’Neill is the restaurateur du jour and makes a very fine ratatouille. His Maison François in St James’s is a mecca for the smart set – Emma Pilkington and Piers von Westenholz are regulars. He and his homoeopath wife, Lydia, spend weekends in the Cotswolds.

Professor James and Joanna Calder

James is the Fortius Clinic consultant foot and ankle surgeon whom those in the know have in their address book. His skill in the theatre is as peerless as his bedside manner. When he isn’t wielding his scalpel, or at dinner parties in Hampshire with wife Joanna, he loves an adventure, recently traversing Jordan’s Wadi Rum desert by camel for charity.

Ben & Jemima Goldsmith

Ben’s passion for the environment is well documented; his glamorous wife, Jemima, is the co-founder of Wild by Tart, one of Belgravia’s finest foodie spots. Pack your swimsuit if you’re invited to Cannwood – they love a daily dip in the freshwater pond.

Robert and Diana Hardman

Literary It man Robert has been toasted at the American Embassy in London and met President Trump in the Oval Office and at Mar-a-Lago. Glamour and politics meet at his launches, where Diana holds court. Come summer, find them in Turkey or Greece.

Tyger Ritblat and Edie Jones

They’re the art-world/nightlife crossover society can’t get enough of: Tyger is the woman behind Tramp’s new-era glamour, Edie’s the buzzy Saatchi Yates gallerina charming collectors and cool kids alike. From Mayfair openings to 5am dancefloors, they rarely miss a party…

The Marquess and Marchioness of Salisbury

London Defence Conference chairman Robert and his wife, Hannah, preside over Hatfield House. The formal garden is alongside the Old Palace, where Elizabeth I held her first Council of State. Hannah is a keen gardener, which is just as well, as the garden is 42 acres.

Lord and Lady Benyon

As Lord Chamberlain, Richard Benyon is a calming presence in the Buckingham Palace picture gallery for those awaiting a gong. He and his wife, Zoe, live at Englefield House in Berkshire, near the Middletons.

Dame Caroline Michel

A veritable literary super-agent with a career ranging from Random House to Granta, Dame Caroline is arguably the lady of letters – symbols, too (having studied Sanskrit). She also helmed the Hay Festival (or as Bill Clinton called it ‘The Woodstock of the Mind’) until 2014. All in all, ultra-glamorous, hyper-connected and currently holding hands with Sir Tim Rice.

Sir Tristram Hunt and Juliet Thornback

Need a few insights into design for living? Meet polymathic Tristram: the former Labour shadow minister runs the V&A; his wife, the homeware brand Thornback & Peel. They radiate éclat. Both were invited for a ‘dine and sleep’ at Windsor Castle with the King and the Queen.

Nicky and Lady Jasmine Dunne

Nicky is chairman of Heywood Hill, the Mayfair bookshop beloved of the Queen; Jasmine, daughter of the Duke of Devonshire, is passionate about the performing arts. At weekends, you might find them at Chatsworth, staying with Jasmine's brother William Burlington and his wife, Laura.

Viscount and Viscountess Grimston

Rosie Grimston helmed a decade-long masterclass in restoration at 18th-century Gorhambury, the family home for 375 years, which she shares with her husband, Jamie. A fine blend of trad and modern, just beyond London, it has hosted Sophie Winkleman, eventer Lissa Green and others for ‘fireside talks’ on matters of huge personal interest.

Marina Lund

A highly respected financier, the CEO of Longview Capital also plays golf, loves tennis, entertains in great style and looks immaculate in Chanel. One of London's most sought-after invitations is dinner on her garden terrace in Westminster, where guests dine to the peal of the nearby abbey bells.

Jonah Freud and Lily Allen

Lily's new beau, Jonah, lives up to his intellectual lineage, ruling over London's literary scene with Reference Point, his library-bar-bookshop. You'll find the pair there, or at nearby Simpson's in the Strand, or in the Cotswolds, weekending with his father, Matthew, at Burford Priory.

Stephen and Christine Schwarzman

Wealth pours into diverse causes from American Anglophile financier Stephen and his ex-lawyer wife, Christine. He received an honorary knighthood for philanthropy and loves a pint in a Cotswolds pub. His smart Hyde Park Corner apartment and Wiltshire pile are desirable invitations: see his Gainsborough and Reynolds. Admire, too, his newly opened Centre of the Humanities in Oxford.

The Hon Vere Harmsworth and Alexandra Wood

The Oxford-educated son of Viscount Rothermere, and heir to The Daily Mail and General Trust, Vere met his fiancée on a horseback safari in Kenya. He and Zimbabwe-born Alexandra now live in Notting Hill. Expect a wedding this summer.

Sir Alan and Lady Parker

An entrepreneur and major charity fundraiser, the chairman of Brunswick is a skilled salmon fisherman, too. An entertaining draw at dinner, he lives in the Cotswolds with his wife, Jane, who's a passionate farmer – there'll be plenty to discuss, then, over dinner with near-neighbours, Jeremy Clarkson and Lisa Hogan.

Stella Jones and Elliot Donald

She has the cool factor – her best (and lifelong) friend is Lila Moss – and is rock royalty, thanks to her father, The Clash's Mick Jones. So it makes sense that her beau, Elliot, is none other than the grandson of Sir Paul McCartney.

Alexandr Wang

A self-made billionaire at the age of 24 with London-based Scale AI, Alexandr, now 29, self-describes as ‘Beep boop making things for the computer.’ He does a bit more than that as chief AI officer at Meta. When he's not on the cover of Forbes, you'll find him rubbing shoulders with global leaders like Emmanuel Macron and Narendra Modi.

Lady Rogers

The world's best-connected restaurateur. Who else could get the likes of Cate Blanchett and Kamala Harris on her podcast? At her famed River Café, staff gave up marking guests in the reservation book as ‘friends of Ruthie’ (FOR) or ‘friends of friends of Ruthie’ (FOFOR) – almost everyone was.

William Bracewell and Andy Monaghan

Dancer, gardener, sculptor, model… Royal Ballet principal William can do it all. He and his (also green-fingered) partner, Andy of Matthew Bourne's New Adventures dance company, are embracing their latest artistic challenge: a northeast-facing garden at their new house in Twickenham.

Sir David Hare and Nicole Farhi

From Grace Pervades to Teeth ‘n’ Smiles, London's theatreland is awash with the work of septugenarian playwright Sir David. To woo him, offer chablis and Dover sole – and spot his fashion-designer-turned-sculptor wife, Nicole, in pride of place at all his opening nights.
