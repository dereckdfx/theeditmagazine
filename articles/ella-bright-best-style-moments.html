From Off Campus to Couture Week: cover star Ella Bright's best style moments

A star on the rise: Ella Bright made her name playing a college student in cosy knits. So how is she navigating her red carpet wardrobe?

Por Edit · 28 de julio de 2026

Bang on trend both on and off the silver screen, Off Campus star Ella Bright graces Edit's September issue cover in suitably elegant regalia, dressed in a powder blue haute couture gown and high jewellery by Dior. Having built up substantial Gen-Z fanfare for her portrayal as Hannah Wells in the much-adored Prime Video series – Bright is truly a star on the rise. But how does her personal style shape up when attending red carpet premieres and A-list events from here to the Big Apple?

Last summer, Ella Bright was sitting her A-levels in London. Now she’s the breakout star of Off Campus, with a legion of global fans obsessing over every scene online. The 19-year-old actress meets Edit as she makes her Paris Couture debut to discuss her extraordinary year

It's simple: captivating cool-girl cuts that pair 1990s slick with a 2026 edge. Case in point? Her inaugural appearance at Paris Couture Week earlier this summer, where the screen queen donned stonewash, boot-cut jeans with a white tank top and black blazer (more Dior). It was delightfully pared back with a palpable cooler-than-you panache.

Other hits include her colour-pop Miu Miu ensemble at Prime Video's inaugural Obsessed Fest, and her canary-yellow sateen mini dress courtesy of Emilia Wickstead as seen out and about in New York. Playful tailoring seems to be her modus operandi (see the striped Thom Browne situation at another Prime party and the tasteful Tory Burch during a US press tour), while column gowns with perfectly nipped-waists in midnight hues are certainly chic during her premiere red carpets.
