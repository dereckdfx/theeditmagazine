Weddings

The best weddings of 2025: from Palazzo parties to the crowning of a new countess

Tying the knot this year? Look no further than these perfect 2025 weddings for the ultimate Big Day inspiration…

Por Edit · 8 de mayo de 2026

Whether it's crowdsurfing grannies, dogs at the altar, or a 160-strong conga, some weddings really took the biscuit last year. While not everyone can host ‘the Met Gala on water’ (though Lauren Sanchez and Jeff Bezos certainly pulled off quite the coup in Venice), these red carpet stars, European royals, and society swans prove that, when it comes to your big day, sometimes the motto truly is: go big or go home.

Harry Bromley-Davenport & Rose Bertie6 September 2025

For their nuptials at the bride’s ancestral home of Allanbay Park, Rose Bertie and Harry Bromley-Davenport summoned up a visual riot: ‘We wanted the whole vibe to be fun and colourful,’ Rose tells Edit. (She set the tone with vivid orange invitations, and this eye for detail was rewarded when a video of their decor went viral on TikTok.) Before dinner, Rose changed out of her Emma Victoria Payne dress into a bespoke gown designed by her friend Grace Fitzalan-Howard. And then hedonism took hold: fillet of beef and fries gave way to baby Guinness shots; the Faithettes performed amid tables on which guests danced; bagpipes blared and even the grandparents joined in the crowdsurfing.

The Earl of Brecknock & Dinah Hillsdon19 July 2025

A sunny July day saw a new Countess of Brecknock come into being at the Church of St Peter and Holy Cross, just a stroll from where Dinah and her swain first crossed paths while walking their dogs Rocky and Crumpet. Dressed in a riot of multicoloured frocks and hats, 240 guests gathered in a marquee on milord’s Wherwell estate in Hampshire, where the father of the bride proposed Katy Perry’s anthem should be renamed ‘I Kissed an Earl and I Liked It’. Ladies Arabella Unwin and Mary Fagan were among the lakeside revellers.

Simon Walsh & Eleanor Ingilby31 May 2025

Eleanor Ingilby’s marriage to Somerset-born Simon Walsh unfolded over two days at Ripley Castle, the bride’s 14th-century family seat in Yorkshire. And while the Earl and Countess of Mansfield mingled with the likes of Lady Justice King and a favoured old ghillie who’d been flown in from the isle of Jura, tradition met a decidedly mischievous streak. When the bride’s father lost his voice, Lady Ingilby stepped in and delivered the speech with gusto. Friday saw singing waiters coax the couple into leading a 160-strong conga through the stable. Saturday descended into dubstep – including a saxophonist doing The Worm – and dancing that refused to die.

Leopold Glover & Phoebe Knatchbull15 February 2025

Phoebe Knatchbull – a scion of the Mountbatten family and related by marriage to the Royals – tied the knot with British banker Leopold Glover in February at the storied Kensington and Chelsea Register Office. The bride embraced modern glamour in a tuxedo-style dress (with ‘Mrs Glover’ discreetly embroidered into the back) from her sister Daisy’s Knatchbull label; and confetti rained down on the steps before celebrations moved to Daisy’s Savile Row shop. Here, a drinks party stretched into the early hours. And the groom managed to lose his wedding band before the night was out.

The Duke of Arjona & Bárbara Mirjan4 October 2025

A church steeped in House of Alba history was the scene for the 4th Duke of Arjona’s wedding to his partner of almost a decade. The 62-year-old groom, a former Olympic equestrian, arrived in a dashing Real Maestranza de Caballería de Sevilla uniform, accompanied by his daughter Amina. Bárbara, 29, followed in a horse-drawn carriage, wearing a Navascués gown with a dramatic trailing veil and – a previous gift from her future husband – art deco-inspired earrings.

Jeff Bezos & Lauren Sánchez27 June 2025

Venice was the setting for the so-called ‘wedding of the century’ when tech billionaire Jeff Bezos and former journalist Lauren Sánchez staged three days of celebrations. These started with a party at the 14th-century Madonna dell’Orto church, where a thunderstorm cast operatic drama over a guestlist that read like a global power index. (Queen Rania of Jordan, Bill Gates, Orlando Bloom, Kendall and Kylie Jenner were all ferried along the Grand Canal.) It was like ‘the Met Gala on water’, quipped one reporter. And Sánchez set the style agenda. She stepped out of the Aman hotel in Dior, McQueen and Schiaparelli before exchanging vows – in a Dolce & Gabbana couture gown – with Bezos in an amphitheatre on the island of San Giorgio Maggiore.

Jean-Philippe Denizot Elzaburu & Marta Elorduy Tourné5 April 2025

Alta sociedad gathered in Toledo when Jean-Philippe Denizot Elzaburu (from a branch of the Bourbon-Two Sicilies family) married Marta Elorduy Tourné at the Church of Hospital Tavera, with Prince Kubrat of Bulgaria and his wife Carla Royo-Villanova in the pews. The bride wore a minimalist gown by Spanish designer Inés Martín Alcalde, offset with a diamond choker and carried a simple bouquet of sunflowers.

Leopoldo Maduro Vollmer & Princess Marie Caroline of Liechtenstein30 August 2025

Cathedral bells rang through Vaduz in August as the London-based heir to the Liechtenstein throne, Princess Marie Caroline (wearing a family heirloom Habsburg Fringe Tiara), married old Harrovian investment manager Leopoldo Maduro Vollmer. Archduchess Marie-Astrid of Austria was among the European nobles who turned out in force.
