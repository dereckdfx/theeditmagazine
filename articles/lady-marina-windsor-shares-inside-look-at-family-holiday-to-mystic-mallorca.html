Royals

Lady Marina Windsor shares inside look at family holiday to mystic Mallorca

The granddaughter of the Duke of Kent joined her new husband and her sister, Lady Amelia Windsor, on a sun-drenched summer holiday

Por Isaac Bickerstaff · 21 de septiembre de 2026

They’re a family with plenty to celebrate this year, and now, Lady Amelia Windsor and Lady Marina Windsor have enjoyed a sun-drenched getaway to Mallorca.

Lady Marina, who tied the knot with Nico Macauley in June, joined her husband, her sister, and her brother, Lord Downpatrick, on their Spanish holiday, taking to Instagram with a set of golden-hour photographs from the royal vacation.

Instagram content

‘Our treasured family pilgrimage into mystic Mallorcan skies and Balearic beauty,’ the granddaughter of the Duke of Kent wrote in the caption, sharing a series of snapshots with the Windsor clan.

The first image shows her soaking up the sun with Lady Amelia, who is said to be engaged to her long-term boyfriend, Ollie Lewis. In characteristically low-key style for the woman dubbed ‘the most beautiful royal’, Lady Amelia has neither confirmed nor denied the news, but the pair have been looking loved-up at events including Wimbledon and Glastonbury Festival since they made their public debut in 2023.

Elsewhere in the social media photo album, Lady Marina shared a video of someone paragliding into the sunset, as well as a photo of her father, the Earl of St Andrews, with her husband Nico and Lord Downpatrick, gathered with glasses of wine and some tapas. There were also a number of selfies with her new husband, a snapshot of Lady Amelia in a perfectly Spanish bikini and sarong, and some picturesque seaside photos – including one that looks to be of this summer’s solar eclipse.

The tight-knit family sent their love in the comments, with Lady Amelia writing, ‘the best, miss it already’ and Lord Downpatrick singing the praises of the ‘olibobs’.

There will have been plenty of time to plan Lady Amelia’s wedding on the holiday – if royal nuptials are indeed on the cards – and to reminisce about the glorious June day when Lady Marina Windsor tied the knot in a romantic service at All Saints' Church, Hovingham,, followed by a reception at Hovingham Hall.

Fortunately for those who missed out on an invitation, art historian Flora Vesterberg was on hand to capture the day and shared candid snaps on Instagram. As a granddaughter of Princess Alexandra, the Hon. Lady Ogilvy, and a great-granddaughter of the Duke and Duchess of Kent, Flora is a distant cousin of the bride. She was joined by a who's who of minor royals: Lady Gabriella Windsor (daughter of Prince and Princess Michael of Kent), Lady Margarita Armstrong-Jones (daughter of David Armstrong-Jones, 2nd Earl of Snowdon, and Serena, Countess of Snowdon) and George Gilman (the Duke and Duchess of Gloucester's son-in-law), all gathered to toast the happy couple.

The bride looked resplendent in a custom gown by sustainable designer Larissa Von Planta, which she paired with a lace veil crafted using heirloom Hapsburg lace. Her ‘something blue’ and ‘something borrowed’ were the same exquisite piece of jewellery: her mother's sapphire brooch worn as an elegant necklace. For her ‘something old’, Lady Marina Windsor opted for treasures previously owned by her grandmother, the late Duchess of Kent, who died in September 2025: the Duchess' diamond and pearl flower earrings and the Kent Pearl and Diamond Fringe Tiara.
