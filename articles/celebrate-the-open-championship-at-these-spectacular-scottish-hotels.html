Celebrate The Open Championship at these spectacular Scottish hotels

Golf was born in Scotland. The glamour soon followed at sumptuous hotels well above par

Por Edit · 15 de julio de 2026

Dornoch Station, Sutherland

Behold one of Sutherland's most sensational courses, the Royal Dornoch: a place of rugged beauty, framed by epic views of the rolling coastline of Dornoch Firth. Equally alluring is this black-and-white limbered hotel, which exudes old-world glamour, sensitively brought up to speed. Aside from the swirling carpets, soft lighting and flashes of paisley print, its prime location is the highlight – it's right next to the first tee. There's no chicer place to stay when visiting this historic golf course, which has been drawing power players since 1877, thanks to its vast ampitheater-like space , mesmeric light and pristine grass. It must be said, though that The Golden Gorse restaurant is worth the trip alone – namely, for the steamed Shetland mussels and roasted Orkney Scallops. marineandlawn.com

Gleneagles, Perthshire

Everything is ‘just so’ here. Set in the rolling hills of Perthshire, The Glorious Playground – as Gleneagles is known – has hosted both the Ryder and Solheim Cups. The illustrious King's course has been, since its creation in 1919, one of the world's most beautiful spots to play. Start the day with a sensational breakfast spread – the creamiest scrambled eggs, crispily delicious bacon – before setting off to one of the estate's three championship courses. After a strenuous few rounds, head to the spa for a relaxing hot-stone massage, or enjoy the indoor pools, steam room and saunas, followed by a dinner of beef wellington and old-school flambéed classics in The Strathearn restaurant. gleneagles.com

The Fife Arms, Braemar

Here it's all so bijou and bespoke… Your partner wants to play gold; you want to study poetry by Burns beneath a Picasso? Only the truly jaded could be bored at The Fife Arms. Every bedroom is assiduously decorated according to its own individual theme. No less fascinating are the ghillies, who can take you salmon fishing, or to play a round of golf at Braemar Golf, the wildest – and highest – 18-hole course in Scotland. For more relaxed courses, there are Ballater and Aboyne nearby, and for those looking for championship-worthy spots, Panmure and the old Course at St Andrews are possible to visit on a day trip. Back at the hotel, enjoy a post-gold dram of some of the rarest, and finest, whiskies at Bertie's, which is home to more than 500 bottles. thelifearms.com

Marine Troon, Ayrshire

Instagram content

Marine is the landmark hotel in Troon, with a strange magnetism that makes it difficult to leave. Perhaps it's the Balmoral-esque aesthetic. It remains a top player in the world of golf hotels after an implausibly lovely restoration, further enhanced by an elegant sauna and a menu of Ishga spa treatments to work through. But stray you must, to sample some of Ayrshire's most mythical gold addresses, namely the Old Course at Royal Troon, which has hosted The Open 10 times – a thrillingly challenging course, thanks to the bracing sea breeze and 18 holes of increasing difficulty. Later, as the sun sets, there is no spot more perfect to enjoy divine miso-caramel-glazed pork belly that goes oh-so well with a Troon Old Fashioned cocktail at the hotel's bar. marineandlawn.com
