Edit Beauty Awards 2026: Best in Hair

The results are in. But which hair products have been crowned in the 2026 Edit Beauty Awards?

Por Edit · 6 de octubre de 2026

Biochemistry and blowdries? When it comes to haircare in 2026, science and style have nevermade a more glamorous pairing. From visionary launches set to transform tresses to futuristicformulas targeting density and growth, these are the innovations leading the way.

1. BEST HAIR MIST

Fabulous for detangling, hydration and shine, this spray is akin to having a halo of Evian-fine mist spritzed over you. With ingredients that read like those in a Cédric Grolet madeleine, including Mirsalehi honey and hydrolysed milk proteins, it makes heat protection up to 230°C a piece of cake.

Honey Milk Active Repair Leave-In Conditioner Mist, £30, Gisou

2. BEST CONDITIONER

Turns out money can buy your hair happiness. This glitzy conditioner comes remarkably close to a salon gloss treatment, leaving hair looking expensively shiny. With an ingredient list spanning safflower oil to hyaluronic acid, it draws moisture into the hair shaft, with a sunny, floral scent reminiscent of yachting trips around Portofino.

Money Laundering Glossing Conditioner, £28, Colour Wow

3. BEST FOR ETERNAL YOUTH

Watch hair become visibly thicker via this peppery, rose-scented serum. Indispensable for irritated and dry scalps, it calms and soothes skin with ginger and amla extract, while vitamin B12 and tetrapeptide aim to slow hair loss and contribute to denser, stronger hair over three months.

Revitalising Fortifying Serum,£200, Sisley Paris

4. BEST SHAMPOO

This crystal-clear shampoo smells so fresh and light, it’s like you’ve bathed in the crystalline waters of an Alpine lake after a storm. With a cashmere-soft wash, it leaves hair super clean without stripping it of nutrients, partly from the blend of fermented rice water, sea kelp bio-ferment and copper peptides.

Bio-Ferment Renewing Shampoo, £51, Kat Burki

5. BEST BRUSH

Consistently delivering and living up to its name, the Tangle Teezer brush is pain-free, whether dry brushing or combing through wet tresses after showering. On both fine and thick hair, it detangles, smooths, and opens up cuticles without unnecessarily pulling.

The Ultimate Detangler, £15, Tangle Teezer

6. BEST FOR HAIR TRANSFORMATION

Ten years in the making, this hair longevity serum was worth the wait. A few drops onto the scalp feel like blasts of oxygen, with users reporting major improvements in density and much less shedding. It also claims to reduce greying after three months.

FutureIQ Biomimetic Hair Longevity Serum, £110, K18

7. BEST FOR INTENSE HYDRATION

A soufflé-like formula that uses an advanced conditioning system to nourish hair within three minutes, leaving it soft, light and beautifully scented with jasmine and honeysuckle.

Pitta Repair Mask, £42, Chāmpo

8. BEST FOR SHINE

For a profound crown chakra experience and silkier strands, this herbal oil is blessed with the botanical powers of bhringaraj, reportedly a game-changer for hair growth, while softening strands with amla and sesame oil and liquorice root powder.

Bringaras Invigorating Scalp & Hair Oil, £60, Kama Ayurveda

9. BEST FOR SUN CARE

If you’re committed to preserving your hair from city smog and summer sun, this spray (which smells divinely of mango) is rich in beta-carotene and vitamins that help create a natural defence barrier against UV rays. It tames frizz throughout the day and is wonderfully refreshing, thanks to morete, sacha inchi and ungurahua oil.

Hydration Detangler + UV Barrier, £36, Rahua

10. BEST PIÈCE DE RÉSISTANCE

More height in your chignon or a little more bounce in your blowdry? These ethically sourced clip-in hairpieces will be the new crowning glory in your styling regime. They’re available in 12- or 18-inch lengths in 15 shades and grip easily into your hairline at the crown with no glue or excessive pinning. Find your perfect shade match from either a video call or by taking a picture of your hair.

Clip-In Filler Hair Extensions, £75 for a 12-inch set, Curated

11. BEST FOR SPLIT ENDS

Princess Diana’s former hair stylist, Sam McKnight, knows hair health comes first. This lightweight styling and conditioning cream, enriched with vitamin B5, shea butter and keratin, works wonders on smoothing flyaways at the root (as well as protecting new growth) while tending to damaged ends.

Happy Endings Nourishing Balm, £22, Hair By Sam McKnight

12. BEST FOR STRENGTHENING

Sometimes you need to dig deep. This all-natural hair vitamin mix aids with inflammation, hormonal changes or nutritional deficiencies that could be blocking long, glossy hair growth. MSM, biotin and vitamins B12, B5, B6 and C join forces to repair and strengthen hair from within.

Hair Food, £37, Glowwa

BEST TOOLS – FOR CURLS

This hot-air diffuser and hairdryer in one creates and defines soft curls via its Supreme Curl Flow technology. Maintaining hydration while reducing frizz, it can handle even the longest locks.

Diffon Supreme, £149, Bellissima Italia

BEST TOOLS – FOR TRAVEL

A third smaller than the original, this portable hairdryer fits in hand luggage, yet delivers a powerful blowdry via Dyson’s Air Multiplier technology. As essential as your passport.

Supersonic Travel Hairdryer, £250, Dyson

BEST TOOLS – FOR STYLING

This styler straightens and smoothes in one stroke, while heat sensors adjust temperature to protect your hair. Also great for creating movement, it’s becoming a styling classic for a reason.

Sensor Protect Styler, £87, Babyliss
