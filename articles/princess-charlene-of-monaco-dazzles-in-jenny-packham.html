Royals

Princess Charlene of Monaco dazzles in Jenny Packham to celebrate her husband, Prince Albert II, at a glittering gala in Monte Carlo

The glamorous Monégasque princess delivered a style masterclass in the brand beloved by the Princess of Wales

Por Isaac Bickerstaff · 21 de septiembre de 2026

Princess Charlene of Monaco took a leaf out of the Princess of Wales’ style playbook at a dazzling gala evening in Monte Carlo on Friday. The ever-glamorous Monégasque royal joined her husband to celebrate the 20th anniversary of his Prince Albert II Monaco Foundation at the Salle Garnier of the Opéra de Monte-Carlo, donning a dress that caught the attention of royal watchers everywhere.

Jenny Packham was the order of the day for Princess Charlene, who was the picture of regal elegance in a Bond girl-worthy beaded maxi dress from the designer, who is beloved by royals the world over, from Queen Mary of Denmark and Crown Princess Victoria of Sweden to Princess Elisabeth of Belgium.

The great and the gilded gathered at the Monte Carlo gala to celebrate the work of guests-of-honour Monica Bellucci and Richard Gere, as well as the efforts of the Prince Albert II Foundation, which has endeavoured to protect the climate since 2006.

It’s not the first time that Princess Charlene was worn a gown by Jenny Packham. The former Olympian wore a Grecian-style number from the brand’s Spring 2026 collection when she accepted an award at the Bal de Noël last December, drawing comparisons to Jenny Packham’s most famous royal wearer – the Princess of Wales herself.

From the early days of her royal life to her Caribbean royal tour, Catherine knows she can trust the sleek gowns and touch of glitter Packham brings to make her feel her best when the eyes of the world are on her. Whether it is delivering Hollywood glamour at the James Bond: No Time to Die premiere or channelling regal chic in navy blue for a charity gala, the pair have long been a match made in royal style heaven. Just refer to the icy blue gown she wore for the German state banquet at Windsor Castle last year, or even the outfit she chose when she posed outside the Lindo Wing after giving birth to Princess Charlotte.

And it seems a host of other Princesses have taken notes. First up was Princess Madeleine of Sweden, who donned a strikingly similar ice-blue gown to the Nobel Prize ceremony in 2025. The daughter of Queen Silvia and King Carl XVI Gustaf, and her husband, Christopher O’Neill, joined senior members of the family, including Crown Princess Victoria and her husband, Prince Daniel, to celebrate the newly crowned Nobel Laureates at the Blue Hall of Stockholm City Hall.

It was then the turn of Princess Catharina-Amalia, who wore Jenny Packham to welcome the Finnish President and First Lady to the Royal Palace in Amsterdam that week. In addition to the pearlescent ball gown, the future queen of the Netherlands made her debut in the Dutch Diamond bandeau, a tiara reserved for the most senior Dutch royals, ever since it began its life in 1879 as a gift from the people of the Netherlands to Princess Emma of Waldeck-Pyrmont, on the occasion of her marriage to King William III.

The very next day, Princess Charlene joined her children, Prince Jacques and Princess Gabriella, for the traditional ‘U Cavagnëtu’ picnic at Princess Antoinette Park. The Princess donned a white dragonfly-adorned dress from Erdem for the annual event, held for the Monégasques, beaming as she stood alongside her twins, who were on their first royal engagement since starting school two weeks ago.

Princess Charlene’s week has, as usual, been marked by exquisite ensembles and a number of glamorous galas – business as usual, then, for the Monegasque royal.
