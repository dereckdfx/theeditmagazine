Royals

Mike Tindall opens up about family life with his three children, joking: 'If they’re as competitive as their mother, we’re doomed!’

Zara Tindall's competitive spirit won her an Olympic silver medal and the British Open. It seems as though Mia, Lena, and Lucas might well follow in her footsteps.

Por Isaac Bickerstaff · 5 de octubre de 2026

With two sporting legends for parents, it seems as though the Tindall children have a very active home life. Opening up about raising Mia, Lena, and Lucas with his wife, Zara Tindall, Mike Tindall revealed that the brood are no strangers to a spot of rough-and-tumble at their Gatcombe Park abode.

‘Even if it’s just a race down the stairs to be the first in bed, there will be tackles at ankle level,’ joked Mike, who has endured his fair share of tackles in his rugby career, which saw him captain the England team: ‘One day, it’ll end with a broken leg, and they’ll learn a good lesson.’

The royal was addressing the crowd at the Henley Literary Festival during a conversation with his ‘The Good, The Bad & The Rugby’ podcast co-hosts. ‘Let’s hope the children become like us,’ he told Alex Payne and James Haskell, adding with a laugh: ‘On the other hand, if they’re as competitive as their mother, we’re doomed!’

Indeed, Zara Tindall has maintained her keen competitive spirit, no doubt inherited from her mother, Olympic equestrian Princess Anne. When Zara followed in the Princess Royal’s footsteps at London 2012, she bagged a dazzling silver medal in the Team Evening competition. The ever-popular royal still competes at the highest of levels, claiming a surprise comeback victory at the British Open in August.

Zara, whose last solo major championship victory came 20 years ago at the World Equestrian Games in Aachen, topped the podium after a stunning show on the cross-country course with her horse, Class Affair. The victory – Zara’s first at the British Open – did not come easily, though. Her performance in the dressage test had landed her outside the top 10, and Class Affair, she admitted, is a challenging ride at the best of times.

And it seems as though she has passed on the sporting passion to her children. Lena, eight, and Lucas, five, can often be seen joining Mike to support Zara on the British equestrian circuit, while Mia, 12, has even started competing herself.

With proud mother Zara watching on from the sidelines, Mia rode her horse, Bunny Two Twirls, at the Heythrop Pony Club Blenheim Palace Show Jumping competition on 20 September. The young Tindall already appeared to be a seasoned competitor, wearing a smart traditional riding uniform and boots with her blonde hair tied up beneath her helmet.

Zara Tindall revealed back in 2019 that both her daughters had a passion for horses. ‘They both ride. Lena is in a little basket on the saddle, purely a passenger. But we just bought Mia a new pony called Magic,’ she told Australian publication Now to Love.

And young Mia’s love of equestrianism goes beyond simply competing. While Zara Tindall has also been busy competing at the Horse Trials, her daughter was spotted grooming her mother’s horse, Classicals Euro Star, between events. The Olympic silver medalist also coached her daughter through some jumps, no doubt offering up her years of competition practice.

Mia Tindall has been joining her parents more and more on the British racing circuit. In April, she wore a very sharp blue pinstripe suit to join Zara and Mike for Ladies’ Day at the Grand National, which came after she kicked off the new year with a trip to Cheltenham alongside cousins Savannah and Isla Phillips.

The Tindalls possess a unique position within the wider family, with the Firm often turning to the clan to bring some levity to the more serious situations. As one source told The Times: ‘The Tindalls are very much the glue of their generation of royals and bring the fun to a family gathering, no matter how serious the occasion.’

Accordingly, Mia Tindall has long been a favourite of royal watchers for her bubbly personality and close bond with the Wales children. Chatting with Princess Charlotte over Christmas walks in Sandringham and giving Prince Louis a run for his money with her animated facial expressions at the Platinum Pageant in 2022, the young Tindall is sure to bring a bundle of energy to any royal event.

Born in 2014, Mia is the granddaughter of the Princess Royal and a great-granddaughter of the late Queen Elizabeth II, making her 19th in the line of succession. However, she does not hold a formal royal rank. When Mia’s mother, Zara Tindall, was born in 1981, her parents, Princess Anne and Captain Mark Phillips, declined to give her a title.
