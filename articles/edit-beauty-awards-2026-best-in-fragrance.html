Edit Beauty Awards 2026: Best in Fragrance

The Best Fragrance winners of the 2026 Edit Beauty Awards have been revealed…

Por Edit · 6 de octubre de 2026

Whether you’re a jet-setting socialite, a city sophisticate or a country-house hostess, your fragrance should be as distinctive as you are. From bright citrus spritzes to smouldering florals, uncover your new signature scent for the season…

1. DREAMY ESCAPE

Here’s the perfect scent to wear island-hopping. The sun is scorching, the water glistens and from you wafts a divine blend of rose and hibiscus. Inspired by the taste of hibiscus tea in a gemstone market, this fragrance is deliciously fruity, with a powdery hit of damask rose, softened by leather and vanilla. Sweet but never cloying, it’s irresistible.

Hibiscus Mahajád extrait de parfum, £205 for 50ml, Maison Crivelli

2. NATURE NOTES

Escape to a forest just after rainfall with the resinous, woody notes of this subtle and elegant scent. Mediterranean pine and eucalyptus meet the harmonious tones of a floral bouquet and leafy vegetation. Uplifting and lush.

Atelier des Fleurs Sous Les Pins eau de parfum, £231 for 150ml, Chloé

3. HEADY PETALS

Beloved by millions, J’adore’s iconic floral fragrance is universally flattering. With its new incarnation, Dior has taken the formula up a notch, giving rose and jasmine a honeyed intensity from velvety apricot and heady ylang-ylang. Ideal for an English rose who wants a scent that can enhance her natural elegance.

J’adore Intense eau de parfum, £115 for 50ml, Christian Dior

4. THE SEDUCTRESS

A gloriously dark cocktail bar with seduction in the air: that’s the setting this heady and sensual scent evokes. It mixes orange blossom, jasmine and tuberose with vetiver and patchouli to thrillingly addictive effect. Spritz when you’re in the mood for seduction.

L’Interdit eau de parfum, £125 for 80ml, Givenchy

5. TIMELESS PERFECTION

Famously what Marilyn Monroe slept in, this is as divine as it was in 1921, when it was created for Coco Chanel. With top notes of aldehydes, rose and jasmine and a base of vetiver, vanilla and amber, it’s an intoxicating scent for a woman who wants the best of the best.

Chanel No. 5 eau de toilette, £127 for 75ml, Chanel

6. SICILIAN DRAMA

A citrusy hit of a fragrance from Mr Ford, who crafts a complex scent like no other. Blood orange, orange flower, cardamom and lime are grounded by oakmoss and patchouli. Conjuring the sun-drenched landscape of Sicily, it’s made for sultry summer nights.

Taormina Orange eau de parfum, £220 for 50ml, Tom Ford

7. OUD MOOD

Keeping one move ahead in the fragrance game is what celebrity nose Francis Kurkdjian does best. His latest offering is a contemporary take on oud: velvety notes of peach round off the rough side without, as Kurkdjian says, ‘denaturing’. Result? A fruitier, very wearable version.

Oud Velvet Mood extrait de parfum, £360 for 70ml, Maison Francis Kurkdjian at Harrods

8. RIVIERA WANDERLUST

Clean linen, sea air and a sense of possibility: that is the vision this light and clean perfume conjures, with a dreamy top note of bergamot and that salty scent of holidays. Perfect for Riviera days.

Sea Salt & Bergamot cologne, £125 for 100ml, Jo Malone

9. BEACHSWEPT GLORY

Everlasting flower mingles with sandalwood in a scent as rejuvenating as the breeze that blows from the beaches of the Mediterranean. Woody, floral and spicy all at once, it’s sunshine on skin in fragrance form.

Contre-Jour by Annick Ménardo eau de parfum, £295 for 100ml, Frédéric Malle

10. URBAN SCENE-STEALER

With top notes of coffee and aniseed and base notes of cocoa and caramel, this is darkly delicious. Made for city nights and low-lit spaces, it’s an addictive scent that will linger long after the party is over.

Strange Heavens Out of the Blue 25 per cent perfume concentration, £490 for 50ml, Clive Christian

11. BLUSHING YOUTH

Perfect for a bright young thing coming into her own, this light and airy scent combines bright citrus and neroli with orange blossom and sambac jasmine. It’s pretty and unashamedly feminine – just the perfume for a budding It girl.

Athénaïs eau de parfum, £270 for 75ml, Parfums de Marly

12. MINIMALIST DELIGHT

The olfactory equivalent of a perfectly crisp white shirt. Freshness comes courtesy of timur, pink pepper and zesty bergamot and lemon; restrained elegance from vetiver. It’s classic yet fresh and wonderfully versatile.

Wild Vetiver eau de parfum, £310 for 100ml, Creed

13. ANDROGYNOUS OFFERING

A classic woody scent for men and women with a delicious harmony of cinnamon, clove, pink pepper, iris, geranium, sandalwood and amber. It’s fabulously nuanced – and so intriguing, you’ll never tire of it.

34 Boulevard Saint Germain eau de parfum, £180 for 75ml, Diptyque
