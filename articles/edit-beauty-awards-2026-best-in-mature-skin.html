Edit Beauty Awards 2026: Best in Mature Skin

From microalgae to polynucleotides, the Edit Beauty Awards Best in Mature Skin winners are the ultimate elixirs for ageing beautifully

Por Edit · 6 de octubre de 2026

Seeking the fountain of youth? A carefully curated, consistent skincare routine is non-negotiable. These tried-and-tested treatments and tools prove precision, potency and science-backed formulas are key to nurturing healthy vitality.

1. BEST AT-HOME TREATMENT

This two-step regime comprises an exosome shot serum and overnight mask. Discipline is rewarded with a firmer, pepped-up complexion. How? Microspicules create tiny channels on the skin’s surface, boosting cell turnover and helping the exosomes supercharge repair.

Exosome Face Lift 4-Week At-Home Treatment Set, £655, 111Skin

2. BEST FOR LUMINOSITY

Smooth on this pale-green cream, then rinse off for quite the transformation: a plumped complexion and feel-good glow. It’s enriched with bioactive microalgae, silica and minerals from Iceland’s geothermal Blue Lagoon.

Algae Mask, £80, Blue Lagoon

3. BEST EVERYDAY MOISTURISER

Talk about one and done! With its slim tube and neat pump dispenser, this is a handy on-the-go hydrator that layers perfectly with make-up. Plus, encapsulation technology ensures the broad-spectrum SPF 50 filters protect your skin but don’t block pores.

Age Defence Moisturising Day Cream, £92, The Skin Diary

4. BEST ULTRA-CONCENTRATED SERUM

This beauty insiders’ essential has a super-charged formula to help accelerate cell renewal. Formulated with medical-grade regenerative plant exosomes and phyto-peptides, it visibly reduces fine lines and boosts luminosity.

The Elixir, £435, Augustinus Bader

5. BEST FOR EYES

The fragile eye area is the first feature to visibly age. This serum’s neuropeptides, biomimetic growth factor peptides and antioxidant glutathione work in tandem to produce quite literally eye-opening results.

Neuropeptide Eyelid Lift Serum, £95, Perricone MD

6. BEST FOR IMPROVED SKIN TONE

For DIY microneedling, this formula packs 10,000-plus peptide-filled hyaluronic-acid crystals, suspended in a clear gel-like serum, into each 30ml bottle. Expect a mild prickly sensation as you massage these microscopic crystals into your skin, creating pathways for the hyaluronic acid and PowerPeptide-5 to target smile lines and crow’s feet.

Need.L MicroPeptide Needle Power Serum, £85, LAB 31b

7. BEST CULT MOISTURISER

There’s a reason one of London’s most sought-after skin therapists relies on this cream as a final flourish to her red-carpet facials. With antioxidant-rich Japanese purple rice, peptide-packed red algae and hyaluronic acid, it instantly softens and plumps skin – with the added bonus of protection against pollution and stress.

The Dewy Skin Cream, £69, Tatcha

8. BEST DEVICE

Harnessing the power of three lasers in one device, this almighty piece of tech is engineered to ‘switch skin into youth mode’ – yet is disarmingly simple to use. No pain, no downtime, and an immediate improvement in skin laxity and appearance.

Laser PRO, £4,995, LYMA

9. BEST FOR RADIANCE

Want that French-girl aura of nonchalant natural beauty? Reach for this antioxidant oil, rich in botanical active ingredients (red marine seaweed and chicory root complex). Luxurious in texture and formulated with the new tightening agent Easyliance, as well as brightening Neurophroline, it makes the complexion radiant.

Glow Elixir, £135, Odile Paris

10. BEST FOR PIGMENTATION

Silky and hydrating, with 4nB (4-n-butylresorcinol), a brightening agent that targets sun damage and dark spots, this two-step treatment smooths texture and visibly fades unsightly pigmentation. No wonder beauty experts in Australia (where the UV index is fierce) recommend it.

Enlighten Gold L* + Ultimate A Gold 5*, £160, E.S.K

11. BEST FOR SKIN LONGEVITY

Medik8’s latest game-changing serum is inspired by polynucleotide treatments. Prismatic PDRN is designed for deep skin penetration and optimal skin absorption, supporting skin recovery. It gives the complexion next-level luminosity, while soothing visible irritation.

Exo-PDRN Prismatic+, £79, Medik8

12. BEST POLYNUCLEOTIDE

Make space for the prettiest bottle in your bathroom. Containing polynucleotides derived from salmon DNA, plus Rodial’s trademark RejuveNAD and Epitensive, the oil preps skin with the collagen-supporting benefits of ‘salmon sperm’. Just add a few drops at the end of your routine.

Salmon DNA Water Oil, £150, Rodial

13. BEST REJUVENATOR

This velvety cream has beauty enthusiasts over 55 hailing a new ‘bounce’ to their complexion. Powered by a pure form of Urolithin A to recharge mitochondria and target visible ageing, the Absolue Longevity MD collection starts with Anticipate (for ages 35 and under), followed by Intercept (35-55), then Reset (over 55s). Get going as soon as you can.

Absolue Longevity MD Reset The Cream, £150, Lancôme

14. BEST FOR TIRED SKIN

A go-to fix for dry or tired skin, as well as recovery from exposure to harsh weather or aggressive cosmetic treatments, this mask’s bio-cellulose material adheres silkily to allow for deep penetration of the clinical-grade, ultra-nourishing formula.

Hydrating Bio-Cellulose Silk Mask, £80 for five, Skin|Cycles
