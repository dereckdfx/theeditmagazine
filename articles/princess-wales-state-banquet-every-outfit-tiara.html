Royals

Every gown and tiara the Princess of Wales has worn to a state banquet

The Princess of Wales is poised to make her next tiara appearance at the Oman state banquet this month. Could she take inspiration from these looks?

Por Isaac Bickerstaff · 8 de octubre de 2026

The Princess of Wales' next tiara appearance has been announced. Catherine will join her husband, Prince William, in supporting King Charles III and Queen Camilla at the upcoming Oman state banquet – the centrepiece of the state visit by the Sultan of Oman.

King Charles and Queen Camilla are set to host His Majesty Sultan Haitham bin Tarik, Sultan of Oman, from Tuesday 20th to Thursday 22nd October, with a banquet at Windsor Castle on the cards.

His Majesty will arrive in Britain on 19th October, landing at Stansted Airport before he is officially welcomed by Prince William at the Sultan’s residence the following morning. The Sultanate of Oman owns a number of London residences, including Nuffield Lodge, 35 Upper Brook Street, and the embassy at 167 Queen’s Gate.

The Duchess of Kent married the Duke of Kent and dutifully served the crown – before becoming a school teacher. Hugo Vickers went inside palace circles to paint the definitive portrait of an extraordinary royal, who died one year ago today

The Sultan will then make his way to the Royal Dais on Dachetr Road in Windsor, where he will be officially welcomed by King Charles and Queen Camilla as the sound of a Royal Salute is fired from the Home Park and the Tower of London. The trio will then join the Prince and Princess of Wales for a carriage procession to Windsor Castle – as has become tradition in recent state visits.

Nigerian state banquet, March 2026

For the Nigerian state banquet, the Princess of Wales broke from the norm to wear an emerald green gown by Singaporean designer Andrew Gn. The finishing touch? Sapphire and diamond earrings that belonged to the Queen Mother and her favourite Lover's Knot Tiara, March 2026

German state banquet, December 2025

The Princess of Wales wore a Jenny Packham gown and the Queen Victoria Oriental Circlet tiara for the state banquet hosted at Windsor Castle in honour of the President and First Lady of Germany, December 2025

American state banquet, September 2025

The Princess of Wales wore a Phillipa Lepley gown and her favourite Queen Mary's Lover's Knot tiara for the state banquet thrown in honour of President Donald Trump, September 2025

French state banquet, July 2025

The French state visit saw Catherine attending her first state banquet since her cancer diagnosis, with the royal mother-of-three wearing a custom-designed red evening gown by Sarah Burton for Givenchy. She completed the look with Queen Mary’s Lover’s Knot Tiara, July 2025

South Korean state banquet, November 2023

The Princess of Wales surprised royal watchers by wearing the Strathmore Rose Tiara for the state banquet for President Yoon Suk Yeol of South Korea. She opted for a custom Jenny Packham gown for the occasion, which she paired with lengthy white opera gloves, November 2023

South African state banquet, November 2022

For her first state banquet as the Princess of Wales, Catherine wore a dazzling Jenny Packham gown which featured glittering embellishments across the shoulders and an elegant cape detailing. She paired the look with her favourite tiara, the Lover's Knot. The outfit would go on to be immortalised on a portrait commissioned for Edit 's July 2024 cover

American state banquet, June 2019

Then-the Duchess of Cambridge, Kate Middleton also wore an elegant white gown to attend the state banquet for Donald Trump's visit in 2019. She paired the Alexander McQueen dress with the Lover’s Knot Tiara, June 2019

Dutch state banquet, October 2018

Catherine's third state banquet was in 2018, and honoured King Willem-Alexander and Queen Maxima of the Netherlands, with the Princess choosing a blue Alexander McQueen gown and her favourite tiara for the occasion, October 2018

Spanish state banquet, July 2017

Kate Middleton chose to wear a pink lace gown by Marchesa for the state banquet for King Felipe and Queen Letizia of Spain, pairing the look with the Lover's Knot tiara, July 2017

Chinese state banquet, October 2015

The then-Duchess of Cambridge attended her first state visit in October 2015 when Chinese President Xi Jinping was hosted by Britain's Queen Elizabeth II at Buckingham Palace. She wore a vibrant red Jenny Packham gown for the outing, choosing the Lotus Flower Tiara
