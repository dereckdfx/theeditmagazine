Royals

Who is Lady Marina Windsor? The Duke and Duchess of Kent's granddaughter, who is celebrating her birthday after a rollercoaster year

She prefers to keep out of the public eye, but has recently weathered the death of her grandmother and celebrated her royal wedding. It's safe to say that everyone is talking about Lady Marina Windsor, who turns 33 today

Por Isaac Bickerstaff · 30 de septiembre de 2026

It was all eyes on Lady Marina Windsor this summer, as the granddaughter of the Duke and Duchess of Kent tied the knot with Nico Macauley, great-grandson of William Berry, 1st Viscount Camrose, on 20 June. For the royal family, the wedding was a chance to celebrate one of their own, just days after the nuptials of Peter Phillips and Harriet Sperling on 6 June. But who is the little-known royal whose boho chic big day made headlines?

The eldest daughter of the Earl and Countess of St Andrews, Lady Marina Windsor was born on 30 September 1992 in Cambridge. Her father is the son of the Duke and Duchess of Kent, making him a cousin of King Charles, while her mother, historian Sylvana Tomaselli, is a descendant of an Austrian-Italian noble family. A sister to Lady Amelia Windsor and Edward Windsor, Lord Downpatrick, Lady Marina is named for her great-grandmother, Princess Marina of Greece and Denmark. She was christened in January 1993 at the Chapel Royal of St James' Palace, with her godparents named as Lady Ralph Kerr, Katherine Ruth Panter, William Hanbury-Tenison, and Sasha Poklewski-Koziell.

Marina attended St Mary's Ascot School, where she was Deputy Head Girl, before going on to study French and Portuguese at the University of Edinburgh, where she starred in a number of plays and wrote for student-based Y-Magazine on ‘interesting causes around the world’.

After graduating from university, Marina went on to work for a number of charities including Veterans Aid, Beat Routes Charity, Eco-nnect and The Big Give, where she is currently Philanthropic Executive. The royal keeps a relatively low profile, living until recently in a ‘cosy flat’ in Notting Hill with her sister, and she has expressed an interest in working in foreign diplomacy in the past.

Until 2008, Lady Marina was 25th in line for the throne, though she forfeited her succession rights alongside her older brother after the pair were confirmed in the Catholic Church, following in the footsteps of her mother and the Duchess of Kent and in keeping with the Act of Settlement 1701. Two years later, she and her siblings modelled for Hardy Amies in Edit.

Lady Marina Windsor announced that she was engaged to Nico Macauley on 8 June 2025. The date of the announcement was especially poignant, coming on the anniversary of her grandparents’ wedding. Sadly, the Duchess of Kent died three months after the news, with Lady Marina and Lady Amelia attending her funeral in September.

Lady Amelia Windsor was amongst those who were quick to share their best wishes with Lady Marina on her Instagram post, writing, ‘Yippeeee!’ Meanwhile, her brother Edward Windsor, Lord Downpatrick, commented simply, ‘So the hazing begins.’ Zenouska Mowatt wrote, ‘Love youuuuuu my babes,’ whilst Flora Vesterberg added, ‘Love you! Happiest news xx’

She gave followers an insight into her hen party with a series of behind-the-scenes Instagram snaps in March. The bridal party celebrated Lady Marina with a weekend on the Isle of Sheppey.

‘Heart bursting for my magical hens and this spectacular weekend,’ she wrote in a caption. The royal certainly dressed the part, donning a pink cowboy hat and a bridal gown for the celebrations, which saw her wear a boho chic style top and skirt for trips to the pub and walks among the verdant landscape.

On the guest list? Friends including Seni Crofts, Talitha Fosh, Amy Gatehouse and Gemma Sort Chilvers. The gang appear to have driven to the getaway together, and at one point gathered around a table as Lady Marina read messages on pieces of paper that sent the group into fits of giggles (artfully muted on Instagram).

And the big day itself was an even more joyful affair. Lady Marina was joined for a romantic service at All Saints' Church, Hovingham, North Yorkshire, on 20 June, followed by a reception at Hovingham Hall.

Fortunately for those who missed out on an invitation, art historian Flora Vesterberg was on hand to capture the day and shared candid snaps on Instagram. As a granddaughter of Princess Alexandra, the Hon. Lady Ogilvy, and a great-granddaughter of the Duke and Duchess of Kent, Flora is a distant cousin of the bride. She was joined by a who's who of minor royals: Lady Gabriella Windsor (daughter of Prince and Princess Michael of Kent), Lady Margarita Armstrong-Jones (daughter of David Armstrong-Jones, 2nd Earl of Snowdon, and Serena, Countess of Snowdon) and George Gilman (the Duke and Duchess of Gloucester's son-in-law) all gathered to toast to the happy couple.

The bride looked resplendent in a custom gown by sustainable designer Larissa Von Planta, which she paired with a lace veil crafted using heirloom Habsburg lace. Her ‘something blue’ and ‘something borrowed’ were the same exquisite piece of jewellery: her mother's sapphire brooch worn as an elegant necklace. For her ‘something old’, Lady Marina Windsor opted for treasures previously owned by her grandmother, the late Duchess of Kent, who died in September 2025: the Duchess' diamond and pearl flower earrings and the Kent Pearl and Diamond Fringe Tiara.

It was a rare display of regal heirlooms from a woman who wears her heritage lightly. It's a new era for Lady Marina Windsor, who will no doubt be surrounded by family – and her new husband – as she celebrates her birthday.
