Edit Beauty Awards 2026: Best in Wellness

2. BEST DAILY RITUAL

This mist is packed with invigorating eucalyptus and punches of peppermint and lavender. Spritz it into running water and your morning shower will feel like a spell in a five-star spa.

Mouth tape, £15, Spotlight Oral Care

Breakfast at Drowsy Silk Sleep Mask, £79, Drowsy
