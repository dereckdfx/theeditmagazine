Royals

When royals and high society gathered for the wedding of Princess Eugenie and Jack Brooksbank

On 12th October 2018, Queen Elizabeth II led guests at the wedding of her granddaughter, Princess Eugenie, to Jack Brooksbank. It was a who's who of high society. Reopen the wedding album on their eighth anniversary…

By Bystander · October 12, 2026

Eight years ago this weekend the eyes of the world were on Windsor for Princess Eugenie's royal wedding to her long-term beau, Jack Brooksbank. The couple were joined by friends and family, including the bride's grandparents Queen Elizabeth II and Prince Philip, Duke of Edinburgh, as they exchanged vows at St George's Chapel, Windsor.

From servant’s daughter to ‘collector of kings’, there was no one like Margaret Greville, whose dazzling diamonds have adorned royal women for over 100 years. On the anniversary of her death, discover the preeminent name in regal jewellery, whose legacy lives on through the likes of Queen Camilla and the Princess of Wales

It was the second royal wedding to be held that year, coming just six months after Princess Eugenie's cousin (and close friend) Prince Harry chose the same venue when he married Meghan Markle, now Duchess of Sussex, in May.

Yet although there were similarities in the guest lists – making it a bumper year for royal wedding watchers – Princess Eugenie's wedding was deeply personal to her and Jack. The bride chose a stunning dress by Peter Pilotto which showed off a scar on her back, a move designed to draw attention to the issue of scoliosis. She wore the dramatic gown with the Greville Emerald Kokoshnik Tiara on loan from her grandmother, the late Queen Elizabeth II. The bridal look was finished with a simple bouquet of white buds and thistles, a colour scheme which was reflected in the outfits of the flower girls and page boys, including Prince George and Princess Charlotte.
