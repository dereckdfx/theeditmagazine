Weddings

When Spain's dapper Duke of Arjona married his radiant girlfriend in a high-society wedding straight from a fairytale

Cayetano Martínez de Irujo, 4th Duke of Arjona, was supported by his aristocratic family as he exchanged vows with Bárbara Mirjan on 7th October 2025

By Isaac Bickerstaff · October 7, 2026

It was a day full of emotion as Spain's Duke of Arjona wed his girlfriend of almost a decade this time last year – at a Seville church with deep-rooted connections to one of the country's most influential aristocratic families.

Cayetano Martínez de Irujo, 4th Duke of Arjona, 63, looked dashing in military dress as he exchanged vows with Bárbara Mirjan, 30, at the church which serves as the final resting place of his mother, the late Cayetana Fitz-James Stuart, 18th Duchess of Alba, once known as the ‘world’s richest woman'.

The House of Alba traces its history back to the 15th century. The late Duchess was a descendent of James II of Scotland and his mistress Arabella Churchill and thought to have been one of the most titled aristocrats in the world. On her death in 2014, her title transferred to her eldest son, Carlos Fitz-James Stuart, 19th Duke of Alba, who was among the guests at his brother's wedding over the weekend.

The Duke of Arjona, a former Olympic equestrian, arrived at the church alongside his daughter, Amina, whom he shares with former wife Genoveva Casanova. The groom looked like a fairytale prince in the uniform of the Real Maestranza de Caballería de Sevilla, which has become the traditional choice for grooms in the House of Alba. Indeed, his nephew Fernando Fitz-James Stuart, Duke of Huescar, wore the same uniform for his wedding, which took place eight years ago this week.

European royals joined the new Grand Duchess Stéphanie of Luxembourg in showcasing their spectacular royal jewels

The bride arrived in a horse-drawn carriage with her father, a Lebanese businessman. She wore a spectacular gown designed by Navascués, featuring an elegant square neckline and dramatic veil that cascaded across the floor as she made her way inside the church. In a touching gesture to her future husband, Bárbara added a pair of Art Deco-inspired earrings he had previously gifted her.

Following the service, the couple departed as man and wife in the back of the horse-drawn carriage and continued their celebrations at Las Arroyuelas, the 1,480-hectare estate the Duke of Arjona inherited from his mother. It formed the perfect backdrop for the festivities.

The Duke of Arjona previously married to Genoveva Casanova from 2001-2007. The couple welcomed twins, Luis Martínez de Irujo y Casanova and Amina Martínez de Irujo y Casanova, before divorcing in 2007. If the name Genoveva sounds familiar it might be because she made headlines in 2023 when she enjoyed a night out in Madrid with King Frederik of Denmark.

Although he is in his 60s, the Duke hasn't ruled out expanding his family. In 2022, Cayetano revealed that he would be willing to welcome a baby with Bárbara if she so wished. ‘I'm open to anything,’ he wrote in his memoir, ‘She deserves it. And if everything continues in the direction it's going… I wasn't open to it before, but now I am. We'll see how things develop .’

Watch this space, it could be that the wedding bells are followed by the patter of tiny footsteps.
