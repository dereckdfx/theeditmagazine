Edit Beauty Awards 2026: Best in Face

Put your best face forward with these supreme products, each a winner of Edit 's Beauty Awards

By Edit · October 6, 2026

From back-to-back social engagements to late-night scrolling, our hectic lifestyles show upon faces first. Thankfully, skincare has evolved to shield stressed complexions and support skinlongevity with clinic-grade innovations, high-tech chronobiology and cortisol-calming ingredients.

1. BEST FACE WASH

With one pump of this daily wash from Soho’s temple to good skin, make-up is swept away with speed and ease. Pomegranate enzymes provide gentle exfoliation while vitamins and antioxidants help calm, hydrate and rebalance the skin.

WORK IT Brightening Enzyme Facial Wash, £34, Skinwork

2. BEST REPAIR CREAM

Consider this a comfort blanket for irritated, sensitive or stressed skin. Squalane, meadowfoam oil, shea butter, rice and camelina oils work with repairing lipids, soothing botanicals and plant antioxidants to reduce tightness and strengthen the skin’s barrier.

The Recovery Cream, £218, Seed to Skin

3. BEST OVERNIGHT TREATMENT

This twice-a-week mask is engineered to release ingredients in step with the skin’s circadian rhythm: exfoliating and rebalancing at bedtime; repairing through the small hours; and nourishing and hydrating before daybreak.

The Exceptional Chronobiology Sleep Mask, £302 for eight doses, Noble Panacea

4. BEST SUNSCREEN

This Australian sunscreen sits beautifully under make-up with an undetectable matte finish, delivering broad-spectrum protection while fighting free radicals and boosting hydration.

To Save Face SPF50+ Superscreen, £35, Mecca Cosmetica

5. BEST DEVICE

Nooānce’s anti-ageing LED mask pairs 50mW/cm2 irradiance with medical-grade silicone that hugs the face like a second skin – right down to the jawline and eye contour. Its 600 red and near-infrared LEDs emit wavelengths that penetrate at different depths to support collagen production for visible improvement.

ELITE X600 LED Face Mask, £599, Nooānce Paris

6. BEST MOISTURISER

Optimise Botox gains with this lightweight cream packed with peptides, niacinamide, ceramides, nourishing oils and a gentle exfoliant – a combination that helps make skin look more even-toned, with fine lines softened.

P-TIOX Cream, £135, SkinCeuticals

7. BEST CLEANSER

Stress doesn’t just live in your head – it appears as inflammation, breakouts and premature ageing. Scented with grounding palo santo and sandalwood, this gel-to-oil cleanser contains a concentrate designed to lower cortisol levels in the skin, support the skin barrier and boost antioxidant defences.

The Caring Cleanser, £76, Neuraé Harmonie

8. BEST PEEL

Beauty insiders have been bringing back this little blue bottle from Japan for years. Gentle enough to be used daily, the serum has a hydrating base and fruit-acid complex that supports natural cell turnover, improving texture, pores and dullness without irritation.

Daily Skin Renewing Soft Peel, £35, Takami at spacenk.com

9. BEST SPRAY

From heatwave commutes to workouts, hypochlorous acid spray is a beauty hack. A spritz can calm redness, soothe flare-ups and blitz blemish-causing bacteria. This one has no added fragrance, no alcohol and the approval of eczema, rosacea and psoriasis bodies.

SOS Facial Spray, £28, Tower 28 at Sephora UK

10. BEST FACIAL PADS

These cult Korean toner pads double as a mini sheet mask for ageing or parched skin. Use them after cleansing to hydrate and soften, or leave on a targeted area for 15 minutes for a glass-skin glow.

Collagen Ampoule Pads, £22, Mediheal

11. BEST EYE PATCHES

Turn a five-minute fix for tired eyes into a designer moment. Hyaluronic acid plumps from within, while escin (from horse-chestnut extract) in the signature Le Lift complex helps de-puff.

Le Lift FlashEye Patches, £86 for 10 pairs, Chanel

12. BEST REFORMULATION

While the cushioning texture and make-up-gripping prowess of Tilbury’s original ‘facelift in a jar’ remain, the updated formula contains barrier-boosting ectoin and a peptide complex to target 10 signs of ageing – and it still delivers that cult instant glow.

Charlotte’s Magic Cream, £79, Charlotte Tilbury
