Edit Beauty Awards 2026: Best in Body

Which products have been crowned Best in Body in the Edit Beauty Awards?

By Edit · October 6, 2026

True beauty connoisseurs extend skincare below the neck. Whether the goal is beautifully buffed limbs, softer hands, or skin so smooth it looks airbrushed, these are the best boosters for your body, from silky hydrators and overnight masks to perfectly portable, easy-glide sunscreen sticks.

1. BEST SELF-TAN

For would-be golden girls who flinch at traditional fake tan, this is refreshingly low drama. Colour develops slowly over a few days, leaving a subtle, sun-kissed glow. Better still, it smells like a beautiful body lotion.

Golden Glow Gradual Tan, £55, Gatineau

2. BEST FOR SENSITIVE SKIN

If fragranced creams leave your skin sulking, this understated lotion is a game-changer. Creamy yet breathable and scent-free, it sinks in without any residue. Marine chitosan, aloe vera and moisturising oils support hydration, making it particularly lovely post-sun.

Body Lotion, £49, ChitoCare

3. BEST ORGANIC BODY OIL

This organic elixir is a thing of beauty for purists and ingredient devotees. Eleven cold-pressed seed oils are infused with botanicals, creating a formula that feels rich yet elegant, with a glorious citrus scent.

Yuzu Noir Ultimate Seed Elixir Organic Body Oil, £33, Nyita

4. BEST BATH OIL

Turn an everyday soak into something more indulgent with this rich, plant-based bath oil. Naturally scented with a blend of rose absolute and five essential oils, it captures the fresh, green aroma of pelargonium Attar of Roses. Vitamin E and sweet almond oil leave skin feeling soft with a delicate, verdant veil of comfort that lingers after bathing.

Geranium No.4 Bath Oil, £39, C.Atherley

5. BEST FOR A DEWY DÉCOLLETAGE

If your facial skincare routine stops abruptly at the jawline, take it down a notch. Rhode’s cult milky essence feels fresh and weightless, layering easily beneath richer, more firming creams without any tackiness. Skin is left looking dewier, while ceramides, glycerin and antioxidant ingredients help support lasting hydration.

Glazing Milk, £20, Rhode

6. BEST LIGHTWEIGHT LOTION

When traditional creams feel too rich, this water-light lotion comes into its own. The barely-there texture vanishes almost instantly, delivering refreshing moisture, while the juicy fragrance – sweet guava with a sunny softness – smells like summer in a bottle.

Body Badalada Skin Refresh Water Lotion, £26, Sol de Janeiro

7. BEST FOR FIRMING

Rich but not sticky, this silky, unscented formula balances bodycare with a skincare-led approach. Caffeine, goldenrod and botanical extracts help contribute to a smoother silhouette, and skin appears more even and nourished over time.

The Shaping Body Cream, £100, Iräye

8. BEST HAND CREAM

There’s something wonderfully ‘Grandma’s dressing table’ about this hand balm – from the illustrated aluminium tube to the grown-up citrus aroma. Scented with yuzu and lemon, the formula is rich yet fast-absorbing, with niacinamide, apricot kernel oil and shea butter leaving hands supple and soft for hours.

Lemon Brightening Hand Balm, £17, Bronnley

9. BEST FOR DIMPLE-PRONE SKIN

Love the original Elemis Marine Cream? Meet its body-focused sibling, which cocoons the skin in moisture. Marine actives, including chlorella vulgaris and padina pavonica, ferment lend the formula its skincare credentials, contributing to a smoother, bouncier and more radiant appearance over time.

Pro-Collagen Marine Body Cream, £70, Elemis

10. BEST SUNSCREEN

This clear, gem-toned stick feels satisfyingly nostalgic – think a Push Pop, but make it an SPF. The smooth surface glides over high points and hard-to-reach parts of the body, laying down broad-spectrum SPF 50+ protection in a clear, comfortable finish. Leak-proof and endlessly portable, it makes sunscreen top-ups feel unusually chic.

Sun Rx Stick, £50, Skin|Cycles

11. BEST OVERNIGHT TREATMENT

A little of this feather-light body mask goes a long way, gliding over skin like a serum. The aloe vera, squalane and algae-infused formula leaves skin feeling softer by morning, while white tea and lotus lend a fresh, spa-like scent.

The Ritual of Karma Overnight Body Mask, £18.90, Rituals

12. BEST GLOW-GETTER

Silky and serum-like, Joonbyrd’s glaze leaves skin glossed rather than glittered. Formulated with grape flower exosomes, peptides, antioxidant-rich botanicals and multi-weight hyaluronic acid, it pairs high-tech credentials with a soft peach, banana and cacao scent.

Future Romance Body Glaze, £62, Joonbyrd

13. BEST RITUAL

Some body products are there to get the job done; others encourage you to slow down. A nourishing base of argan, moringa, coconut and watermelon seed oils leaves skin softly conditioned, while the scent – citrus peel, warm woods and soft florals – feels gently transporting. A drop through the ends of the hair doesn’t go amiss either.

Sacred Union Scent & Dry Oil, £88, Mauli

14. BEST CLINICAL BODYCARE

Here’s a sophisticated formula combining ceramides, squalane, panthenol and kelp extracts in a cream that feels distinctly more treatment than moisturiser. The texture is beautifully refined and skin feels smoother, cushioned and exceptionally well cared for.

Bio-Rejuvenating Body Lotion, £84, Kat Burki
