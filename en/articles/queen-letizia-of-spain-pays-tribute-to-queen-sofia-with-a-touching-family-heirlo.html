Royals

Queen Letizia of Spain pays tribute to Queen Sofia with a touching family heirloom brooch

The ever-glamorous royal joined her husband, King Felipe, for a gala dinner in Finland on Tuesday

By Isaac Bickerstaff · October 7, 2026

There were no tiaras on display for Queen Letizia and King Felipe of Spain’s state banquet in Finland on Tuesday, but that’s not to say that the royals didn’t put on an impressively glamorous display. President Alexander Stubb and First Lady Suzanne Innes-Stubb hosted the King and Queen for a grand dinner at the Presidential Palace in Helsinki, the culmination of a three-day state visit to Finland.

Birgitte is on a major four-day tour of the nation alongside her husband, the Duke of Gloucester

As royal watchers have come to expect, Queen Letizia delivered a masterclass in understated elegance. The mother of two donned a silky black gown from Felipe Varela, paired with Massimo Dutti pumps and an Aquazzura clutch. But it was her jewellery that truly stole the show: in particular, a brooch that paid a sweet tribute to Her Majesty’s mother-in-law, Queen Sofia.

This diamond floral jewel (thought to be in the shape of a dahlia) has been in Queen Sofia’s collection since her wedding to King Juan Carlos – back when she was a princess of Greece, and he was Spain’s Crown Prince. According to royal jewellery expert The Royal Watcher, the brooch is thought to have been a present from the Spanish Cortes Generales, and Sofia let out an admiring ‘ahhh’ when she first saw it.

Cayetano Martínez de Irujo, 4th Duke of Arjona, was supported by his aristocratic family as he exchanged vows with Bárbara Mirjan on 7th October 2025

The brooch has accompanied Queen Sofia throughout her entire royal career, for events including the wedding of then-Princess Margrethe of Denmark and Count Henri de Monpezat and the christening of Prince Nikolaos of Greece. Her daughter, Infanta Cristina, wore the piece in 2010 to attend the wedding of Crown Princess Victoria of Sweden.

Despite this proud history, the brooch is rarely seen in public nowadays. Queen Letizia has worn the piece as Princess of Asturias at the Save the Children Foundation gala in 2007 and also during a visit to Britain in 2019, when she pinned it to her lapel for the Service of the Order of the Garter. Perhaps Her Majesty chose the piece for this state visit to pay tribute to her mother-in-law, who has spent the year grieving for her sister, Princess Irene of Greece.

Nor was this brooch the only piece of jewellery that Queen Letizia wore to the Finnish gala this week. She completed the ensemble with a pair of heirloom jewels that once belonged to Queen Victoria Eugenie, a granddaughter of Queen Victoria. The diamond earrings and diamond bracelets were part of the Queen’s vast collection, much of which was gifted to her upon her wedding to King Alfonso XIII of Spain. The wedding gift comprised the joyas de pasar collection, which is passed down between the Queens of Spain to this day.

Queen Letizia put on a style showcase the morning before the gala dinner. A vision of pink perfection, she paired her Carolina Herrera coat with a dress from Pedro del Hierro for a visit to Käpylä Public School and a lunch with the Mayor at Helsinki City Hall. The royal completed her ensemble with a pair of Gold & Roses star earrings in rose gold and her Magrit Carlota pink pumps. If the vivid pink coat looked familiar, royal watchers might recognise it from Queen Letizia’s state visit to Denmark in 2023 or her meeting with Martin Scorsese the following year.

The previous day, she joined King Felipe for a reception with representatives of the Spanish community in Finland, wearing a playful outfit that will have certainly turned heads among the crowd.

Her Majesty put her characteristic spin on sleek tailoring, wearing a Pertegaz black blazer-and-trousers set adorned with rhinestones and boasting some impressively feathered sleeves. According to the brand, the whimsical ensemble embodies a blend of ‘comfort and luxury, functionality and femininity’ – a perfect piece, then, for Queen Letizia, who can carry off modern elegance just as well as she wears royal jewels.
