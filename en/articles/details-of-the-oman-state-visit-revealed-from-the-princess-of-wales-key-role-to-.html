Key details of the Oman state visit revealed: from the Princess of Wales' key role to the guests at the Windsor Castle banquet

King Charles and Queen Camilla are hosting the Sultan of Oman for a state visit later this October

By Isaac Bickerstaff · October 8, 2026

The Princess of Wales will play a key role in the upcoming state visit from Oman, Buckingham Palace has revealed. King Charles and Queen Camilla are set to host His Majesty Sultan Haitham bin Tarik, Sultan of Oman, from Tuesday 20th to Thursday 22nd October, with a banquet at Windsor Castle on the cards.

His Majesty will arrive in Britain on 19th October, landing at Stansted Airport before he is officially welcomed by Prince William at the Sultan’s residence the following morning. The Sultanate of Oman owns a number of London residences, including Nuffield Lodge, 35 Upper Brook Street, and the embassy at 167 Queen’s Gate.

The Sultan will then make his way to the Royal Dais on Dachetr Road in Windsor, where he will be officially welcomed by King Charles and Queen Camilla as the sound of a Royal Salute is fired from the Home Park and the Tower of London. The trio will then join the Prince and Princess of Wales for a carriage procession to Windsor Castle – as has become tradition in recent state visits.

Upon arrival in the Quadrangle of the Castle, the royals will be given a Royal Salute by a Guard of Honour while the regimental band plays the Omani and British National Anthems. The Sultan and King Charles will then inspect the Guard before all five royals view a military parade. This will be followed by a lunch in the State Dining Room, with other members of the royal family, and a special exhibition of Oman-related items in the Green Drawing Room.

The culmination of the state visit will come that evening, when King Charles and Queen Camilla will host the Sultan for a state banquet, with both the King and the Sultan delivering speeches. Buckingham Palace has revealed that other members of the royal family will also be attending the banquet, and in recent years, the Princess of Wales has donned a tiara to join the likes of Prince William, Princess Anne, the Duke and Duchess of Edinburgh, and the Duke and Duchess of Gloucester.

The following morning, the King and the Queen will bid farewell to the Sultan, who will then make his way to London to meet with Prime Minister Andy Burnham at Number 10 Downing Street. But there is more royal pomp and ceremony to come, as His Majesty is then invited to join the Duke of Gloucester for a Guildhall banquet hosted by the Lady Mayor.

On Thursday, the Sultan will conclude his British trip with a visit to Here East at the Queen Elizabeth Olympic Park, where he will learn more about place-based transformation and the regeneration projects following the London 2012 Olympics. After a showcase of energy and renewables, he will depart for Oman from Stansted Airport.
