Edit Beauty Awards 2026: Best in Grooming

Want to know the best grooming products this year? Look no further than the winners of the Best in Grooming Edit Beauty Awards

By Edit · October 6, 2026

It’s not easy being a man about town. From late-night dinner parties to Addison Lee air-con,cosmopolitan life plays havoc with skin and hair. Face the music and product-up: from creatine inyour moisturiser to serum infused with peptides, it’s time to give your routine a workout.

1. BEST SHAMPOO

This detergent-free cream is a dream multitasker, cleansing and conditioning in one step to hydrate less-than-lustrous locks and maintain natural oils while leaving hair feeling fresh, clean and healthy. No lather – and no toxic ingredients, either.

New Wash Original, £48, Hairstory

2. BEST HAIR STYLING SPRAY

How to get beachy waves in Costa del Clapham? Fortunately, like a Baywatch lifeguard, Murdock Barbers of London has come to the rescue with its Sea Salt Styling Spray. It lifts flattened hair with instant volume and restores texture to frazzled not-quite-curls.

Sea Salt Hair Styling Spray, £22, Murdock Barbers of London

3. BEST FOR BULKING

Sick of the same old gym routine? Add a little wildness to your workout with Ancient + Brave’s Brave Ancestral Protein. It’s a cacao blend that delivers 22 grams of protein per serving (from beef isolate, liver and kidney powder) and whole-food ingredients, with the added bonus of bovine collagen and methylated B-complex vitamins. Mix it with milk, water or your smoothie to start mornings the wild way.

Brave Ancestral Protein, £52, Ancient + Brave

4. BEST 24-HOUR MOISTURISER

Sometimes you just don’t have time in the day to reapply. Fortunately, Rituals’ Homme 24h Hydrating Face Cream keeps skin nourished from morning until – well, morning. It contains a potent combination of hyaluronic acid, noted for reducing the appearance of fine lines, and a peptide complex that keeps skin feeling resilient.

Homme 24h Hydrating Face Cream, £40, Rituals

5. BEST RETINOL

First applied twice weekly at night, and then once a day, this hardworking moisturiser combines the intensity of a serum and the silky feel of a cream. With 0.25 per cent retinol, vitamin C and peptides, it aims to stimulate collagen production and target signs of skin ageing.

Collagen ReActivate PM, £115, HydroPeptide

6. BEST GROOMING TOOL

With stainless-steel blades and five magnetic attachments, this grooming kit is ready for action anywhere. The hair and beard trimmer works wonders on both, while the precision trimmer enables you to style with detail and clean lines. Should you want a close shave, there’s the foil shaver head; the body trimmer picks up the rest, along with the nose and ear trimmer.

5-in-1 Men’s Grooming Kit, £33, BarberBoss

7. BEST REJUVENATOR

Heath, founded by a father and son during their walks on Hampstead Heath, presents a no-nonsense approach to male skincare. This moisturiser was conceived to energise and protect the complexion from the travails of day-to-day life, with creatine and a vegan peptide to leave skin feeling firmer and less fatigued, with no lingering cakey sensation.

Age Defence Moisturiser, £12, Heath

8. BEST HAIR SERUM

Is it a searing Sahara out there? Enter Dior’s Sauvage Hair Serum. It uses the hydrating powers of that ultimate desert survivor, the cactus, to add shine and texture to dry, tired hair, with cactus pulp harvested from the Dior garden in Lanzarote and a subtle smell of Sauvage. Run through your hair for a quick shot of luxury.

Sauvage Hair Serum, £67, Dior

9. BEST HAIR CLAY

Infused with mushroom nutrients, this preparation is supremely versatile. Whether you’re looking for a cool slick-back or bedhead sprezzatura, Ruffians has devised a neat way to help hair look thicker and more voluminous, enabling a wide range of styles with a strong hold that never feels claggy.

Matt Clay, £18, Ruffians

10. BEST SKINCARE SET

With its Core Trio, YCode offers the holy trinity of male skincare for morning, noon and night. Well-Kept Eye Serum, which aims to energise and lift heavy or puffy bags, contains a toned concealer to cover up dark circles. Mattifying Moisturiser keeps skin looking fresh and dulls any excess shine. Finally, there’s the Hydrating Face Serum to rejuvenate and freshen you up, no matter where the day (or night) might take you.

The Advanced Routine, £113, YCode

11. BEST NIGHT CREAM

Geneva-based dermatologist Dr Levy has conjured up the ultimate skincare indulgence. This evening application cocoons the face and neck in a nourishing formula that contains hyaluronic acid, niacinamide and micro-algae extract. It’s comforting and feels rejuvenating – you wake up feeling less stressed and smelling of roses.

Overnight Booster Cream, £280, Dr Levy Switzerland

BEST FRAGRANCES – FOR THE JET-SETTER

This citrus-like cocktail scent infused with cognac essence is an ode to the cellars the perfumer, a direct heir of the Hennessy dynasty, grew up surrounded by. Angels’ Share on the Rocks eau de parfum, £215 for 50ml, Kilian

BEST FRAGRANCES – FOR THE MAN WHO DOES IT ALL

The perfect blend of smoky and sweet, this masculine classic, perfect for work or play, has been ‘the one’ since its original launch as an eau de toilette in 2008. The One for Men eau de parfum, £83 for 100ml, Dolce & Gabbana

BEST FRAGRANCES – FOR THE CHARMER

There are the usual leathery notes, but the real star here is the maninka fruit opening, carrying the tart sweetness of passion fruit and the subtle decadence of strawberries and cream. Bottled Beyond eau de parfum, £105 for 100ml, Boss
