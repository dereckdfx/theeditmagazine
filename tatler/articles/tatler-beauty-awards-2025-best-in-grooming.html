Edit Beauty Awards 2025: Best in Grooming

The ultra-effective producs for the man about town

By Astrid Joss · September 26, 2025

A gentleman never tells – but we simply must. These ultra-effective products for the manabout town promise discreetly brilliant results. From the latest male-targeted age-defying skincare and gadgets (hello, Benjamin Button) to an anti-hair-loss innovation from celebrity trichologist Philip Kingsley, these are the best a modern male dandy can get.

1 BEST SHAMPOO

This detergent-free cream formula cleanses and conditions in one go for a head of thick, healthy hair, without stripping the scalp of natural and necessary oils. It won’t foam, but its super-hydrating action will result in hair that is softer and easier to style, with increased volume and shine. New Wash Original, £48, Hairstory

2 BEST HAIR TREATMENT

For a thicker head of Harry Styles-level hair, the answer awaits in trichologist Philip Kingsley’s latest innovation. Containing an active complex to maintain follicle condition and protect density and quality over time, it’ll have hair looking fuller and healthier with just three months of continuous use. Density Preserving Scalp Drops, £52, Philip Kingsley

3 BEST FOR TEETH

Need a reason to smile? You’ll be grinning like the Cheshire Cat after making this mildly abrasive dental polish part of your daily morning routine. The dual-acting formula works to banish surface stains caused by food, drink and smoking, while its blue pigments offer an instant whitening effect that creates a brightly glowing white optical illusion after brushing. Radiant Amethyst Colour-Enhancing Dental Polish, £65, Mahsa

4 BEST SERUM

For its new male skincare range, Dior turns to the potent active powers of the mighty cactus plant, whose survival skills within the desert are unrivalled. Infused with aqueous extract of cactus to improve the skin’s capacity to absorb moisture, cactus pulp to hydrate and cactus oil to strengthen and nourish the skin’s barrier, it’s a hat trick for male skincare. Sauvage The Serum, £70, Dior

5 BEST FOR ENERGY

Feeling listless, fatigued and generally over it? Seek out the wellness brand adored by celebrity nutritionists and television personalities alike for a full reset. Its expertly formulated creatine powder, enhanced with magnesium, taurine and vitamin D, is designed to increase your energy, aiding in muscle and bone growth, as well as cognitive health. True Creatine +, £22.40 for 30 servings, Ancient + Brave

6 BEST CREAM

Calling all overachievers – Sisley’s high-performing men’s skincare range specifically focuses on how male skin is different to women’s (such as a more acidic pH and higher levels of sebum production). This targeted formula, which includes shea butter, phytosqualane, sunflower oil and a high dose of other nutrients, delivers a soft and supple feel. Sisleÿum for Men Anti-Aging Comfort Cream, £242, Sisley

7 BEST CLEANSER

For the deepest clean that results in the softest skin, turn to this cleanser by Soho-based British skincare brand Skinwork. Apply a small amount of cream – packed perfectly with vitamin E, zinc and aloe vera to cleanse, calm and hydrate – then massage off with a warm face cloth for an at-home spa-like treatment and finish. Treatment Cleanse Cream Cleanser, £34, Skinwork

8 BEST FOR EYES

Keep calm and carry on partying – nobody will detect any sign of tired eyes thanks to thisserum, which has been formulated specifically for men’s skin and is geared to conceal dark circles with its matte concealer finish (available in different shades). Fine lines, puffiness and inflammation will also be visibly reduced as a result of its fast-acting cocktail of green coffee, beetroot, wild ginger and sea salt. Well Kept Eye Serum, £49, The-Y-Code

9 BEST SHAVING DEVICE

Precision is everything when it comes to keeping male facial hair in check. Enter Wahl’s patented Forever blade, hard coated for exceptional durability, that cuts close to the skin’s surface for a thrilling end result. Better still, its three interchangeable heads for trimming, shaving and detailing create a speed-of-light routine. Pro Series High Visibility Beard Trimmer, £170, Wahl

10 BEST TOOL

Turn every day into a spa day with this three-step, clinically inspired MedTech device, which uses contrast therapy and ionic microcurrents to refine and refresh. The 18°C cooling mode depuffsthe face, the 43°C purifying function deep cleans pores and the 37°C infusing function soothes the complexion. The result? A sculpted face and the healthiest of glows. The ThermoGlow Pro, £249,Dr Levy Switzerland

1 The Dandy

An intense yet deeply sophisticated and seductive mix of white florals, sandalwood and bitter almond that gives way to a delicate, bright layer of vanilla tincture, this sweet and flirtatious scent projects confidence and is guaranteed to get you noticed. Vanilla Sex eau de parfum, £290for 50ml, Tom Ford

2 The Jet-Setter

Descending from the French cognac-making dynasty, Kilian Hennessy presents this ode to his family heritage. A rich scent of raspberry liqueur, spices, sweet praline and, of course, intoxicating cognac, it creates a sumptuous, boozy atmosphere that draws you and all around you in. Angels’ Share Paradis eau de parfum, £310 for 100ml, Kilian at harrods.com
