Revisit Pippa Middleton's ‘almost royal’ wedding as she celebrates her anniversary

Princess Eugenie, Roger Federer, and, of course, the Princess of Wales were on the guest list when Pippa Middleton and James Matthews tied the knot in a fairytale wedding nine years ago

By Edit · May 20, 2026

They were predicting rain but the sun peeped its head through the clouds for Pippa Middleton and James Matthews's wedding ceremony at St Mark's Church in Englefield. The rumours were true: the radiant bride wore a bespoke Giles Deacon dress, while page boy and flower girl Prince George and Princess Charlotte stole (rather a lot) of the limelight outside the ceremony, as their mother, the Duchess of Cambridge, attended to her sister's dress. Once their vows were exchanged, Pippa's family - including parents Michael and Carole and brother James Middleton - joined Prince Harry, Spencer Matthews and Roger Federer for a bash back at the family's estate. We can only imagine the dancing going on inside…
