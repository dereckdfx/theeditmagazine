The best of Bystander: Cartier Queen's Cup Polo

Cartier Queen's Cup Polo remains one of the most refined dates of the British summer sporting season – and the 2026 edition was no exception

By Isaac Bickerstaff · June 21, 2026

There is perhaps no summer event as glamorous as the Cartier Queen’s Cup Polo. Taking place at the Guard’s Polo Club, it sees the Royal Family – from the late Queen Elizabeth II and Prince Philip, Duke of Edinburgh, to the polo-playing Prince William – rubbing shoulders with the great and good of art, culture, high society and beyond. While the winners change each year, there are several things you can always bet on: a delicious three-course lunch, unmissable action – and the Cartier bellboys standing proud in their iconic red uniforms. The 2026 edition of the event was no exception. The private garden – newly reimagined under the auspices of new Cartier UK managing director Agathe Delpont – bloomed with flowers in sumptuous shades of cream, white and the softest pink as society swans Lady Eliza and Lady Amelia Spencer held court.
