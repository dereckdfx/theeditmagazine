The Duchess of Sussex returns! But will Meghan Markle reprise her Brit-chic royal wardrobe?

Montecito modern slick, or classic Old Blighty elegance: what will the Duchess of Sussex be wearing when she arrives back in the UK?

By Chandler Tregaskes · August 20, 2026

It was a bombshell sung from the rooftops of Camp Sussex over in sunny California. A telegram of headline-making, society shaking communiqué: Harry and Meghan will return from their Montecito idyll to the United Kingdom in mere weeks. Naturally, they want to send their kids off to British school (hoorah!), and in an announcement so swift and – to many – unexpected following the pearl-clutching furore that was ‘Megxit’ back in 2020, the King only found out on Sunday! Corr blimey! Better pull up a few more chairs around the Christmas table…

The Marchioness of Bute has announced she is closing her eponymous fashion label, Serena Bute London. The clothes – loved by Poppy Delevingne, Rita Ora and so many more – never looked more glamorous than when they were modelled by Serena and her daughter, Lady Lola, at the family seat of Mount Stuart, the staggering four-storey Victorian Gothic fantasy on the Hebridean Isle of Bute

That’s right, this royal news is icy fresh. And while the Sussex's greatest supporters cheer – and their naysayers jeer – the fashion crowd have another, more urgent musing pressed upon their lips: what will the Duchess wear for her return to Great Britain?
