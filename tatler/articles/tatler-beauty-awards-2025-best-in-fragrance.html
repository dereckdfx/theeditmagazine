Edit Beauty Awards 2025: Best in Fragrance

These scents have you covered for all hours and events

By Astrid Joss · September 26, 2025

From garden brunches under poplar trees to late-night afterparties, these scents have you covered for all hours and events. Think fresh citrus that warms into musk, a bold berry-wood chypre for confident It girls, and deep amber-smoky blends that stick with you long after lights go down, taking you from bright debutante to dark enchantress with just a spray.

1 THE MEDITERRANEAN ESCAPIST

Bright, breezy and effortlessly chic, this feels like a sunlit escape to the Italian coast. Crisp Sicilian lemon and apple lead the way, softened by bamboo, jasmine and white rose before settling into a gently woody base of cedarwood and musk. An essential for European It girls. Light Blue eau de toilette, £50 for 90ml, Dolce & Gabbana

2 THE DIAMOND COUNTESS

Imagine walking into a ballroom that’s dripping in velvet, chiffon and chandeliers – ylang ylang and blackcurrant lead into sultry amber and musk, lingering like the aftermath of a particularly mysterious guest… A fragrance for those who don’t just attend the party – they are the party. Royal White Diamond eau de parfum, £2,900 for 75ml, Amaffi

3 THE REIGNING QUEEN

Working women need a scent that’s powerful and refined, and none fit the bill more than Goddess.A gourmand blend of vanilla balanced with lavender, it’s warm, soft and sophisticated, and feels polished yet inviting enough for daily wear. Goddess eau de parfum, £138 for 100ml, Burberry

4 THE PLAYFUL SPARK

This fragrance is pure nostalgia in a bottle – an airy, sunlit, delicious scent that evokes a joyous childhood memory. Think juicy peach and tutti-frutti with white musks and a whisper of vanilla-sandalwood cream. It opens bright and candy-sweet, like a bag of Haribo, but morphs into a cosy, soft musk that lingers like a hug. Kurky eau de parfum, £185 for 70ml, Maison Francis Kurkdjian

5 THE GARDEN HOSTESS

Notes of mandarin and pink pepper pop like champagne at sunrise, followed by a lush trio of Centifolia and Damascena roses, peony and lily of the valley. As the fragrance settles, a scent of cashmere wood, vanilla and musk begins to emerge. A floral finishing touch for a gala or garden stroll – elegant, yet impossible to ignore. Eladaria eau de parfum, £265 for 75ml, Creed

6 THE ENCHANTED ROSE

Perfect for a confident doyenne, from the boardroom to an intimate candlelit evening. A luminous, romantic scent that marries luscious fig and regal rose with an earthy, woodsy core, the result is velvety and grounding, reminiscent of a cherished heirloom perfume with a modern edge. LBTY Zephirine eau de parfum, £225 for 100ml, Liberty London

7 AIRS AND GRACES

An It-girl essential by day or by night, this fragrance has a bright, tart top note of miracle berry and bergamot that opens into a heart of ginger lily, supported by oak and patchouli. It’s a woody,skin-like chypre that balances strength and softness, offering a fresh yet mysterious presence. Barénia eau de parfum, £140 for 100ml, Hermès

8 FEMALE FANTASY

When a garden party calls, this is impossible to beat. A tender blend of citrus-lit green florals that settles into a soft musk, it evokes the comfort of a loving embrace. Clean, powdery and effortlessly refined, without overt sweetness. Eau Parfumée Thé Vert eau de toilette, £174 for 150ml, Bvlgari

9 THE ROMANTIC

For any young socialite on the scene, this airy floral of white peony and lily of the valley, combined with radiant stephanotis, is ideal for a Mayfair date night. Gentle yet suggestive, it’s a comforting, intimate scent with subtle depth. Tears from the Moon eau de parfum, £265 for 100ml, Gucci

10 THE SOCIETY GIRL

Nothing says chic like a subtle scent that still sings. Bergamot and jasmine flirt with cyclamen, orange blossom, sandalwood and blonde woods. For the Chelsea girl who knows an enchanting smell is as essential as a good head of highlights. Missing Person eau de parfum, £99 for 50ml, Phlur

11 THE DARK MISTRESS

The black bottle is as intriguing as its contents. White musk and suede flow into patchouli, rose and sandalwood, with a twist of cumin. Layers of pure and carnal elements create a compellingly rich, dark and velvety scent – not to be worn lightly. Carbone eau de parfum, £145 for 50ml, Balmain

12 THE TEMPTRESS

Not to be worn by the faint-hearted, this fragrance is pure femme fatale. Opulent Turkish rose and patchouli meet at the top, unfurling into a base of amber and incense. A complex journey – enveloping yet graceful. Portrait of a Lady eau de parfum by Dominique Ropion, £290 for 100ml, Frédéric Malle
