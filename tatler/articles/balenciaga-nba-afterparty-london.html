Balenciaga, basketball and one sceney Sunday basement party: What happened when the historic French Maison touched down in London to shoot hoops and sip champagne in Mayfair?

This just in: Basketball is the hottest sport in the world right now. Romeo Beckham and girlfriend Kim Turnbull just proved it…

By Chandler Tregaskes · January 19, 2026

London, January 18th. A majority of the nation are tucked up at home with endless cups of tea on brew as another wintry, sober weekend passes by slowly. Those hibernating house-dwellers are reaping the rewards of a cosy start to the new year, dressed in tracksuit bottoms that should never leave the abode. Aww… how sweet.

Not for the cool-crowd, however. Quite the contrary. On the agenda of the capital’s thrill-seeking, fashion-craving aesthetes, an arrival straight from Paris has lit up London: Balenciaga. The historic French Maison is determined to shake awake those brave enough to slip into Pantaleggings and oversized butterfly shades on the eve of Blue Monday. The house - recently under new sovereignship since the debut of its latest creative director, Pierpaolo Piccioli - is not only expanding its haute horizons, but branching out into the glitzy world of celebrity sporting. Game on!

Awards season 2026’s most glamorous trend has links to Victorian high society. It's time to flock to ostrich feathers

It comes as a new wave of leading style purveyors are latching onto world-class competitions across the globe. Louis Vuitton has teamed itself up with the zooming, heady-with-petrol-fumed Formula 1. Rolex is taking to the seas with the cutting-edge SailGP. For Balenciaga, it’s a new collaboration with the NBA that is bouncing into the big leagues. They shoot, they score, they wear scores of sports-luxe chic on courts from Boston to Brooklyn.
