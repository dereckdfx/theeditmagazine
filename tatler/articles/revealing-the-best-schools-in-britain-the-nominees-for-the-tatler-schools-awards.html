Revealing the best schools in Britain: the nominees for the Edit Schools Awards are in…

From heroic headteachers to unsung legends, the Edit Schools Awards celebrate the crème de la crème of British education

By Edit · September 23, 2026

An interview with Britain's Strictest Head, a tour of international schools and a celebration of 100 years of the Royal Ballet School: Edit's education editor, Tori Cadogan, reveals what to expect from this year's guide, on sale 3rd September with the October issue

Every year, schools up and down the nation wait with bated breath to hear whether they have won a place on the Edit Schools Awards. It's been a rigorous year, with exacting researchers touring some of the most erudite, creative, and athletic institutions in the land. Now, the results are in. Which Prep School reigns supreme? Which Public School Head will claim the crown? And who will be recognised for a lifetime of achievement in educating the next generation?

‘Once again, the loyal Edit Schools’ readers have decided who will be the deserving recipients of the 2026 Edit School Awards,’ says Education Editor and Schools Guide mastermind, Tori Cadogan: ‘With nearly 7,000 votes cast, nominations are hotly contested, and I am absolutely thrilled to announce the 2026 nominees.

What's in a name? That's the question prospective parents might ask of the sister sites to some of the UK's best schools popping up across the world. Edit's education editor jets to the Middle and Far East to discover whether these branded schools truly are A*

‘In truth, all the Edit Schools, their teachers and the unsung heroes (who ensure the smooth running of the schools behind the scenes) deserve to win. To be featured in our Guide, we scrutinise every detail to ensure we draw together a list of the UK’s finest independent schools.’

But winners there must be, and this year, the Edit Schools Awards nominees are as follows. The full winners list will be revealed in the coming days – follow @tatlermagazine across social media for the latest updates.
