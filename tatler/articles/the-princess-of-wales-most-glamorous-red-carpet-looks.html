Royals

The Princess of Wales' most glamorous red carpet looks: from Alexander McQueen to Jenny Packham

British designers have become firm favourites of the Princess of Wales – with a handful of notable exceptions

By Stephanie Bridger-Linning · September 22, 2026

The Princess of Wales stepped out in one of her favourite labels as she joined Prince William at a star-studded film premiere in London on Tuesday night.

Catherine, 44, delivered a masterclass in Hollywood-worthy regality in a flowing deep purple creation by Jenny Packham, the British designer behind some of her most memorable state banquet ensembles over the years. She and Prince William were on hand to show their support for the Royal Film Performance world premiere of Digger, a new film starring Tom Cruise and directed by Alejandro G. Iñárritu.

Prince William and the Princess of Wales joined stars at the London premiere of Tom Cruise's new film, Digger

But, as these photos prove, it is on the red carpet that the partnership between Packham and the Princess really shines. Whether at the BAFTA awards, Royal Variety Performance or attending the annual Royal Film Performance, Catherine often turns to the ultra-feminine – and sometimes glitzy – glamour of this bastion of British design. And the Princess is far from the only royal to fall for Packham's charms: Princess Charlene of Monaco wore the label as recently last week.

Royal Variety Performance, 2014

The Princess of Wales, then Duchess of Cambridge, attended the Royal Variety Performance for the first time in 2014, a year after the birth of her son, Prince George. For the occasion, Kate Middleton opted for a designer with royal pedigree, Diane von Furstenberg. The royal stepped out in the black lace Zarita gown, which featured a fitted bodice, boat neckline and lace sleeves. The style was typical of Kate for that era; now the Princess is more likely to take a sartorial risk. The accessories were simple: a vintage Magid beaded clutch bag and a pair of diamond and blue topaz earrings by Kiki McDonough, which remains one of her favourite jewellery designers.

Royal Film Performance of Spectre at the Royal Albert Hall, 2015

Kate again looked to one of her go-to designers in a bespoke Jenny Packham gown, teamed with Jimmy Choo platform sandals.

UK Premiere of A Street Cat Named Bob , 2016

Kate Middleton opted for a Self-Portrait pleated crochet maxi-dress with a playful leg slit, paired with Gianvito Rossi suede pumps, as she joined celebrities on the red carpet for the premiere of this feel-good film in aid of Action on Addiction.

BAFTA Awards, 2017

For Prince William and Kate’s first BAFTA outing together, Kate wowed in a bespoke Alexander McQueen gown and wavy-cut Prada sandals.

Royal Variety Performance, 2017

The Duchess of Cambridge was pregnant with Prince Louis when she joined Prince William for the Royal Variety Performance at the London Palladium in 2017. She opted for one of her go-to labels for the occasion: Jenny Packham. The Princess of Wales owns several designs by this stalwart of the British fashion scene – this creation was an ice blue, embellished design with a semi-sheer bodice and sweetheart neckline. The outfit was finished with a pair of Oscar de la Renta heels, Jenny Packham clutch and diamond chandelier earrings that once belonged to Queen Elizabeth II.

BAFTA Awards, 2018

The year that the Time’s Up movement loomed large at the BAFTAs, Kate’s black belt, worn over an emerald Jenny Packham gown, was interpreted as a nod of solidarity. Attendees had been invited to wear black as a gesture of support, but Kensington Palace did not comment on the matter at the time

BAFTA Awards, 2019

Kate looked to her go-to, Alexander McQueen for this show-stopping number, matched with glittery Jimmy Choos

Royal Variety Performance, 2019

It was an Alexander McQueen moment for the Duchess of Cambridge in 2019 – a bespoke lace creation that featured black floral lace over a nude fabric, with black velvet trim on the sweetheart neckline and shoulders. Once agin, Kate Middleton chose to compliment the outfit with a pair of stunning earrings, this time by Erdem.

BAFTA Awards, 2020

Guests were invited to dress sustainably for the BAFTAs in 2020, with Kate re-wearing a favourite Alexander McQueen gown, previously seen during a royal tour in Malaysia in 2012. A pair of Jimmy Choo pumps completed the polished took.

No Time To Die world premiere, 2021

The princess dialled up the glamour in a Jenny Packham ‘Goldfinger’ sequin dress with a cape overlay, teamed with Aquazzura pointed pumps in gold.

Earthshot Prize Awards ceremony, 2021

Championing sustainable fashion by dipping back into her wardrobe, Kate opted to re-wear a favourite Alexander McQueen gown previously seen in 2011. She added a pair of elegant Oscar de la Renta pumps

Royal Variety Performance, 2021

The Duchess of Cambridge's dress designer for the 2021 Royal Variety Performance was Jenny Packham – another triumphant outing in the British label. She debuted the emerald green, sequin gown during the 2019 royal tour of Pakistan, and its second appearance was just as memorable. Her earrings were also previously seen on the royal tour two years prior. The Missoma creations were proof that Kate is the queen of mixing high street with high-end design.

Royal Film Performance of Top Gun: Maverick , 2022

The princess was chic in Roland Mouret for the star-studded event, opting for ‘Lamble’ off-the-shoulder monochrome gown, teamed with wavy-cut suede Prada pumps.

The Earthshot Prize Awards ceremony, 2022

In keeping with the evening’s sustainable theme, Kate donned a rented Solace London ‘Sabina’ off-the-shoulder dress in bright green, sourced through the rental platform HURR, paired with Gianvito Rossi embellished pumps.

BAFTA Awards, February 2023

A keen champion of sustainable fashion, Kate opted to re-wear a beloved Alexander McQueen gown that she previously donned at the BAFTAs in 2019. She updated the ethereal white, one-shouldered dress with cascading shoulder embellishments and added black velvet opera gloves, a black Jimmy Choo clutch and Aquazzura gold pumps.

Royal Variety Performance, 2023

The 2023 edition of the Royal Variety Performance was particularly special as it brought together the Prince and Princess of Wales with Crown Princess Victoria of Sweden and her husband, Prince Daniel. Given the exceptionally high profile of the evening, it made sense that Kate Middleton selected to wear something extra special: a custom gown by Safiyaa, a label also loved by her sister-in-law, Meghan Markle. The cobalt blue gown featured a cape and embellished detailing at the neck, meaning the Princess of Wales could forgo a necklace in favour of a pair of sparkly earrings.

Royal Variety Performance, 2025

The Princess of Wales debuted a new, emerald green gown by German designer Talbot Runhof for the Royal Variety Performance in 2025, which she accessorised with the Greville Chandelier Earrings and the Queen Mary Choker bracelet.

BAFTAs, 2026

The Princess of Wales graced the BAFTA red carpet in Gucci, accessorising with diamond pieces from the royal vault.

Digger Royal Film Performance world premiere, 2026

The Princess of Wales looked resplendent in Jenny Packham, accessorising with an amethyst necklace that once belonged to Queen Elizabeth, the Queen Mother, and earrings inherited from Princess Diana
