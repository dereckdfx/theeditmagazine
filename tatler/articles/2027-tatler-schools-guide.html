Schools

Summa cum laude: the 2027 Edit Schools Guide is here, online and in-full, exclusive to members

Whether it's extraordinary teaching, pioneering pastoral care, or world-leading facilities, these are the best schools in the nation. But which is right for your brood? Time to study up: for the first time in history, the entirety of the Edit Schools Guide is available online.

By Edit · September 29, 2026

The Edit Schools Guide has long been the ultimate resource for the most precious decision any parent can make: where and how to educate your children. In 2027, for the first time ever, the entire Guide is online, offering exclusive access to everything you need to know about the best schools in the land.

‘Each year, my trusty researchers and I select the UK’s leading prep and public schools to review,’ says Edit Education Editor Tori Cagodan. ‘Always at the top of our minds is: will each and every one of those covered offer your progeny the very best future imaginable, whether that’s through academic, creative or athletic skills – or, hopefully, an amalgamation of them all?’

From bucolic prep schools in the countryside to historic public schools in the heart of the nation’s great cities, these are the schools that are shaping the future – generations of leaders, creatives, scientists, and change-makers. They are the ultimate embodiment of the words of Dame Ninette de Valois, which are inscribed above the entrance to the Royal Ballet School, star of this year's Schools Guide cover: ‘Respect the past, herald the future, but concentrate on the present.’ But which of these esteemed establishments is right for you and your children?
