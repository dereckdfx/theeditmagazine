Queen Bea! Celebrating 41 of Beatrice Borromeo’s most spectacular looks in honour of her 41st birthday

From princess gowns to classic Dior tailoring, Beatrice Borromeo, wife of Pierre Casiraghi and daughter-in-law of Princess Caroline of Hanover, never fails to impress

By Stephanie Bridger-Linning · August 18, 2026

Beauty, grace and intellectual brilliance: birthday girl Beatrice Borromeo has it all.

The daughter of an Italian count, Beatrice’s well-connected family owns most of the Borromean Islands in Lago Maggiore, including Isola Bella, where Beatrice married the dashing Pierre Casiraghi (son of Princess Caroline of Hanover and grandson of Princess Grace of Monaco) in 2015.

Heading to Oxford? Whether you're a religious rower, a union hack, or a Right Hon, as results day rolls around, here's a totally objective, comprehensive guide to the college you're about to call home

After studying law, Beatrice studied at the prestigious Columbia Graduate School of Journalism and forged a career in both print and broadcast before turning her considerable talents to documentary filmmaking (she recently spoke to Edit about one of her biggest scoops). She is also a mother to Stefano, born in February 2017, Francesco, born in May 2018, and Bianca, born in October 2025.
