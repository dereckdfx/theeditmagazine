Edit Beauty Awards 2025: Best in Hair

These products deliver strength, shine and growth

By Astrid Joss · September 26, 2025

It’s your crowning glory – so give it the royal treatment with products and tools that deliver strength, shine and accelerated growth. Whether you’re deep-conditioning at home in the Cotswolds or Chelsea, or chasing gloss and volume for a night out in Mayfair, these are the must-haves for hair that turns heads

1 BEST FOR LUXE LOCKS

The secret to siren-worthy blowdries at home starts with a spray. The ultra-light, heat-activated, silicone-free formula restores vitality to fine hair by strengthening it and delivering shine – without weighing it down, so expect serious bounce and volume, too. No wonder celebrity stylists swear by it. Dream Coat Supernatural Spray, £27, Color Wow

2 BEST HAIR ACCESSORY

These chic silk snag-free bands are your saviour when it comes to keeping hair knot-free. Excellent for whipping hair up into a high, glossy ponytail, they also work wonders for preserving a blowdry, keeping your hair up and your style intact while you sleep. Skinny Scrunchies, £45, Slip

3 BEST DRY SHAMPOO

Hair flatter than a glass of day-old champagne? Forget the aerosol overload and powdery roots that come with most dry shampoos. This leaves your hair looking just-washed fresh (even after themost intense pilates workout). Even better, its super-advanced, behind-the-scenes science helps balance scalp health and control excess oil production over time. Airwash Dry Shampoo, £48, K18

4 BEST SCALP TREATMENT

This revolutionary scalp serum addresses all three phases of the hair growth cycle. A little goes a long way, increasing volume and shine, and – crucially – promoting healthy hair growth from the inside out, all thanks to a powerful combination of fermented macronutrients, copper peptides and pro-B vitamins. Bio-Ferment Scalp & Hair Treatment, £68, Kat Burki

5 BEST CONDITIONER

A winner for both scent and substance. No matter how much sun-blasting you’ve put your hair through, this soothing plant- and marine-derived conditioner is a godsend. Its combination of fermented rice extract, B vitamins, algae extracts and vegan proteins works magic on hair that’s been stressed by exposure to the sun or styling with heat. Moisture Melt Conditioner, £27, SEA+SOLU

6 BEST FOR DEEP SHINE

Beauty sleep for the tresses is a thing, especially for accelerating hair growth. Oribe’s leave-in night cream soothes and moisturises, and does a massive repair job on split ends. Best of all, it absorbs quickly, so don’t worry about spoiling your Frette pillowcases. Gold Lust Restorative Night Crème for Hair, £65, Oribe

7 BEST FOR HAIR REGROWTH

The best things come in small Tiffany-blue boxes – like this game-changing serum: a roll-on elixir that works to speed up hair growth while injecting life into thin or damaged locks. It’s infused with biotin, peptides and keratin to support you growing visibly stronger, fuller-looking hair. The Hair Serum, £75, Sweed

8 BEST HEAT PROTECTOR

Eliminate bad hair days with minimal effort with this sophisticated formulation. Star ingredients tsubaki and daikon seed oils transform colour-treated hair, moisturise intensively and protect when blowdrying or using straightening irons. Miraculous Oil, £34, Aveda

9 BEST FOR DETANGLING

Give up wrestling with your brush and turn to this game-changer, whose nylon bristles gently detangle the most challenging hair types and help get long, unruly locks under control. It primes and zhuzhes up hair to an extraordinary degree – no routine is complete without it. N.04 The Massaging Detangling Hair Brush, £142, La Bonne Brosse

10 BEST DEEP CONDITIONER

OD’d on straightening irons? Give your hair a holiday with this deep-conditioning mask, which smooths out kinks and hydrates frazzled ends. Working on the hair’s texture to restore elasticity and manageability and going beneath the surface to smooth out frizz, it’s the equivalent of Botox for hair. Elasticizer Deep Conditioning Treatment, from £21, Philip Kingsley

11 BEST FOR TRANSFORMATIVE TRESSES

Like taking your hair on a trip to the Amazon Rainforest. The clever combination of rahua andpalo santo oils not only smells wonderful – it also has a healing effect on the hair, deliveringuplifting and transformative effects to the scalp. Rahua Elixir, £162 for 30ml, Rahua

1 BEST FOR STRAIGHTENING

Want hair sleeker and shinier than an Aston Martin? This masterpiece straightens and dries hair simultaneously, resulting in super-smooth tresses with plumb-line precision, as well as shine and body, leaving your hair in superb condition, without any wispy sides. You’ll be hooked.Airstrait Straightener, £449.99, Dyson

2 BEST FOR STYLING

Whatever your stylistic mood – pre-Raphaelite curls or full Carolyn Bessette-Kennedy with a volumised straight blowout – this tool does it with ease. It reduces frizz and combats dryness thanks to using thermal heat rather than hot air – plus, its lightweight frame and elegant vegan leather bag makes it the perfect travel companion. The Multi-Tasker, £195, Hershesons

3 BEST FOR HAIR GROWTH

This sci-fi helmet kickstarts the natural reparative hair mechanisms by stimulating the production of an energy molecule known as ATP to promote more efficient hair growth. Admire a change in as soon as 16 weeks. Hair Beauty and Growth Helmet, £600, Nooance
