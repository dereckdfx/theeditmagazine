Royals

Stoic Mike and Zara Tindall attend the Laver Cup with Roger Federer following their family grief

Rugby star Mike announced the death of his father, Philip, last week

By Isaac Bickerstaff · September 28, 2026

Mike Tindall has stepped out for the first time following his father’s death, joining his wife, Zara Tindall, at the Laver Cup on Friday evening. The couple seemed in high spirits despite the solemn news, which Mike announced on his podcast ‘The Good, The Bad & The Rugby’.

Known for their sporting prowess, Mike and Zara were in good company at the tennis tournament, rubbing shoulders with Roger Federer and taking photos with players including Casper Ruud, Learner Tien, and Francisco Cerundolo. The Tindalls are said to be close friends with Federer, attending the London screening of his Twelve Final Days documentary in London.

‘Brookes, not books’? Old hat. ‘Dullham’? Forget about it. This year's cohort of freshers has a brave new world of university stereotypes and social scenes to contend with. Fashion-forward Zara, who has topped Edit’s best-dressed list in the past, donned a blue blouse from L’Agence with a Veronica Beard dickey blazer in navy pinstripe, which she paired with a matching set of trousers and an Apspinal of London bag. Mike, meanwhile, looked dapper in a navy blazer and chinos.

The Tindall family have been putting on a brave face since the sad passing of Mike’s father, Philip, who died at the age of 80. Former England rugby player Mike announced the news on 23 September, telling listeners: ‘Unfortunately, my dad passed away last week.’

The former English rugby player went on to thank his father’s former rugby club, which paid him a moving tribute over the weekend. ‘Big shout-out to Otley Rugby Club,’ Mike said. ‘They played Chester on the weekend, and they did a minute’s silence for my dad, who was captain in ‘72-’73. That little moment, I got to watch it. Someone recorded it, sent it to me, and then they wrote a little thing in the programme as well, which I read.’

Philip and his wife Linda – described by Mike as a ‘very stoic, northern lady’ – have remained out of the royal spotlight, but have no doubt been doting grandparents for Mike and Zara’s children: Mia, Lena, and Lucas. Philip Tindall was spotted side-by-side with Princess Anne when Mike and Zara tied the knot in 2011.

The announcement comes four months after Mike opened up about his father’s ‘brutal’ health condition at the Celebrity Golf Classic in the West Midlands. ‘My dad is not doing great at the moment,’ Tindall told Hello! Magazine. ‘Parkinson’s is brutal, and there is no let-up from how it attacks the body and changes people.’

Princess Anne’s son-in-law added: ‘After 23 plus years of the disease, it has quite honestly left him a shell of what he once was, which is why I don’t want anyone else to see a parent or loved one suffer and have their quality of life decline so massively.’

The pair are said to have been very close, with Mike describing Philip as ‘an absolutely unbelievable father’ in previous interviews.

In spite of the heartbreak, the Tindall family have been ensuring that things are business as usual for Philip’s grandchildren, with Mia Tindall joining her mother to compete at the Blenheim Palace International Horse Trials on 19 September.

Royal sources have spoken of how Zara and Mike Tindall can always be relied upon to bring their characteristic sense of good humour to royal occasions. In 2025, an insider told The Times, ‘The Tindalls are very much the glue of their generation of royals and bring the fun to a family gathering, no matter how serious the occasion. They can help take the family’s mind off the gravity of what is going on.'

Elaborating on the Tindalls’ position within the wider family, the source said: ‘While some family members can be very ‘royal’, Mike is just himself and is relaxed and playful with George, Charlotte and Louis. It helps that they all have children around the same age that they can fool around together privately and bring a sense of normality to what is a stressful situation for everyone involved.’

Perhaps the couple are already looking forward to their annual trip to Australia, where they are avid supporters of the Magic Millions polo, which takes place on the Gold Coast in the New Year. Before then, Mike and Zara may well bring their sunny demeanours to Christmas celebrations at Sandringham.
