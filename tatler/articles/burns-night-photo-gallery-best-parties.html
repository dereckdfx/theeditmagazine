Were these the best Burns Night parties of all time?

Get into the Burns Night spirit with a wee dram of Highland high jinks, courtesy of Edit's Bystander

By Bystander · January 23, 2026

Haggis! Clootie dumplings! Whisky, wine... Burns Night is here and whether you were born and raised in the family tartan or are gingerly toasting Robert ‘Rabbie’ Burns by partaking in neeps, tatties and ‘Typsy Laird’ (the finest of sherry trifles), Edit has dipped its toes into the Bystander archives to deliver an intoxicating dram of Highland high jinks with a touch of Dashing White Sergeant and more than an eyeful of tartan to mark the Burns Night celebrations.
