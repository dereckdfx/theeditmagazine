Beauty

A New York minute! Edit jets off to the Big Apple to discover the Amaffi fragrance that proves diamonds really are a girl's best friend

A 24-hour return trip to the New York City? Beauty director Astrid Joss couldn't pass up the opportunity to sample the scent that had Manhattan on lockdown

By Astrid Joss · October 14, 2025

If I told you I hopped on a plane to New York for 24 hours just to sample a fragrance you may well be forgiven for thinking I’m slightly mad. Yet earlier this summer I did exactly that. It was worth every millisecond.

Fragrance to me is the most dreamiest, magical part of beauty. Not only is it something you wear to enjoy and revel in much like your favourite dress or most treasured piece of jewellery but it’s something you ‘journey’ in. It’s intoxicating sensory powers and ability to bring long lost memories is legendary – the mists of time clear within moments when tied to the right scent.

The Beauty Awards are unveiled in the October issue of Edit, with Beauty Director Astrid Joss revealing an insight into the selection process for Edit

So when I got the call up from the ultra-high-end perfume house Amaffi, to be one of the world firsts to try their latest fragrance 'Royal White Diamond' by celebrated perfumer Azzi Glassier it was an irresistable invitation. Known for making powerful (and expensive) scents using meticulously curated blends of rare natural ingredients created over painstakingly long periods of time, they really do pack one mighty aromatic punch. Crafted for a discerning clientele who savour life’s luxuries, Amaffi is clear in its sole pursuit for excellence through it's masterful creative choices. From its boutique location (in London it’s Sloane Street) to only working with the world’s most acclaimed noses who are duly granted an unlimited time frame to work within. How else can a true work of art be produced?

For their latest launch, who better to take up the mantle than British perfumer Azzi Glasser? Known primarily for her unorthodox style along with her vast experience, Glasser has collaborated with designers, musicians, actors and models, including Jude Law and Cindy Crawford. From creating a bespoke scent for Alexander McQueen’s iconic 1999 catwalk show to realising the much revered scent Agent Provocateur Parfum, and even capturing the smell of burning wood and freshly fallen rain – it was a tantalising thought in itself to imagine how she might interpret the titular ‘white diamonds’.

With the dream collaboration in place, there was only one final part of the equation that my scent-filled 24 hour trip required – a touch of drama. As I stepped out of the Four Seasons ready to cross the road to the Amaffi boutique on New York’s fashionable 57th Street, the heavens opened. Even better, the roads had been partially shut down in anticipation of the brand’s then ambassador Momo, from the K-pop phenomenon Twice. A genteel Four Season’s doorman escorted me across the street under an umbrella while I navigated a large gathering of fans who had assembled outside hoping for a glimpse of the pop star beauty. It felt, as New York so often does, like a film set and an entirely befitting moment for Glasser’s diamond launch.

Inside, a small select group gathered to sample Royal White Diamond with Momo at the helm. A captivating and delicately sweet scent, it’s as gentle as it is empowering. Opening with notes of natural ylang-ylang absolute, cassis and the essence of classic muguet, which is embraced by a heart of rose de mai, tuberose with a touch of violet leaf and tonka and base notes of of amber, musk and natural oud. Describing the scent as a ‘beautiful blend of tantalising ingredients that will seduce your heart and soul’, Glasser says, ‘This fragrance will give you the sense of glamour with a couture veil of pure elegance. The mood is sensual, seductive and provocative with a loyal loving soul.’

These scents have you covered for all hours and events

And what could perfect an already exceptional scent? It’s diamond like shaped bottle crafted from hand-polished glass is as enthralling as the fragrance itself. But it doesn’t stop there. Housed in a protective wooden box decorated with Swarovski crystals and lined in red velvet it comes with an 50ml travel-sized bottle so you can travel and wear it with ease.

With Marilyn Monroe once declaring ‘diamond’s are a girls best friend’ and Shirley Bassey celebrating their worth when she sang ‘Diamonds Are Forever’, Amaffi has released their own ode to this perennially evocative gem through Glasser’s incomparable scent. Thanks to Amaffi, there is now more than one way to wear diamonds…
