Bystander throwback: Laura Parker Bowles and Harry Lopes' romantic wedding attended by Prince William and Kate Middleton

Laura Parker Bowles and Harry Lopes tied the knot at St Cyriac's church in Wiltshire on 6 May 2006. Edit 's Bystander covered the society nuptials

By Edit · May 6, 2026

Following a short engagement but a lengthy romance, Laura Parker Bowles and Harry Lopes tied the knot at St Cyriac's church in Wiltshire on 6 May 2006. The service was traditional and conducted by Sally, the local vicar. The bride, stunning in a dress by Robinson Valentine, was escorted by the pages, Tarka Ossian and Rowan Brudenell-Bruce, who spent much of the service hitting best man Duncan Westbrook over the head with their floral weapons. Guests included the Prince of Wales (now King Charles III) and the bride's mother, the Duchess of Cornwall (now Queen Camilla), and Prince William and Catherine Middleton (now the Prince and Princess of Wales). At the reception, guests tucked into fish pie and salad. The cake, made of thousands of chocolate muffins, was cut with Brigadier Parker Bowles's military sword. After Ayesha Shand caught the bride's bouquet, the happy couple sped to Claridge's for the night before heading to Vietnam for their honeymoon.
