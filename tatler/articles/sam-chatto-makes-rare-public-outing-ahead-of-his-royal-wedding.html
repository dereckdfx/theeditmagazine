Royals

Sam Chatto makes rare public outing ahead of his royal wedding

Princess Margaret's grandson set the press ablaze when he announced his engagement this summer – but it's been business as usual for the young artist

By Isaac Bickerstaff · September 30, 2026

Sam Chatto seems unfazed by his newfound spotlight. Princess Margaret’s artist grandson, who made headlines after announcing his royal engagement this July, seems to be preparing for the art world to descend upon London next month, gearing up by attending a private viewing of the Royal Academy of Arts’ new exhibition, ‘Painting the French Riviera’, on 28 September.

Looking dapper in an all-black ensemble, Sam – the son of artists Lady Sarah Chatto and Daniel Chatto – rubbed shoulders with a cultural crowd, including Mia Regan, Batia Ofer and Sir Tristram Hunt at the event, though he was not joined by his fiancée, fellow artist Eleanor Ekserdjian.

Royal watchers have been getting to know Eleanor – the daughter of art historian Professor David Ekserdjian and Susan Moore, an art critic at the Financial Times – ever since she began dating Sam in 2021. The couple, both of whom studied at Edinburgh University, foreshadowed their engagement in Christmas 2024, taking the ultimate step in a royal relationship by attending festive celebrations at Sandringham. Regulars on the social scene (including Edit’s Little Black Book party in 2022), they have since been photographed at the wedding of Peter Phillips and Harriet Sperling.

Sam and Eleanor announced their engagement with an Instagram post on 13 July. ‘I’m really delighted to say that Ellie and I are engaged. And we couldn’t be happier,’ Sam wrote in a caption alongside a photo of the pair together: ‘I proposed with a porcelain ring which I made.’

Flora Vesterberg, the granddaughter of Princess Alexandra, the Hon Lady Ogilvy, was among the first to publicly congratulate the couple, writing: ‘Very happy for you all.’ King Charles was informed of the news and was said to be ‘very happy’. There is still no news on when their royal wedding might take place, though Spring 2027 is purportedly in the pipeline.

An abstract painter, much of Eleanor's work is inspired by the Armenian diaspora, and has been held in numerous private collections, including one at London’s Redfern Gallery. One of her pieces, simply titled ‘SAM’, is an evocative sketch of her royal boyfriend, once displayed at The Gallery at Green & Stone’s summer exhibition.

Muse as well as partner, Sam Chatto is also quite the artist himself. Having found himself ‘completely uninspired by my three-month job working in the commercial art market’, Lady Sarah Chatto’s oldest son took matters into his own hands, and now describes himself as ‘a British artist and maker currently working in clay to create functional and sculptural wood-fired ceramics, from my home and studio in West Sussex.’

An accomplished ceramicist, he last year conducted an apprenticeship in Japan, under the noted potter Yagi Akira. His experience was no doubt behind King Charles inviting him to Buckingham Palace last year for a State Banquet held for the state visit of Emperor Naruhito and Empress Masako of Japan.

And his time in Japan will have left plenty to discuss with Eleanor, who has completed a residency with the Sokyo Gallery in Kyoto. Eleanor is not thought to have any blue-blooded connections, but her career has brought her in contact with royalty before. Another of her residencies was undertaken with Hauser & Writh, the art venue where Princess Eugenie serves as director.

It was their joint outing to Sandringham that really thrust Eleanor into the spotlight as it marked a major break from royal tradition. Kate Middleton, now the Princess of Wales, wasn’t offered an RSVP until she married Prince William, nor was Sophie, the Duchess of Edinburgh, welcomed into the Victorian country house prior to her wedding to Prince Edward. Edoardo Mapelli Mozzi joined his then fiancée Princess Beatrice at Sandringham in 2019, just months before they said ‘I do’. So, King Charles’ decision to extend an invitation to Eleanor Ekserdjian was quite extraordinary.

For now, though, it’s business as usual for Sam Chatto.
